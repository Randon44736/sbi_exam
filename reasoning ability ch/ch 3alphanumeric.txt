1. CORE METHOD & GOLDEN SPEED FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A) The Direction Arithmetic Formula (દિશા સરવાળા-બાદબાકી નિયમ):
   Never count manually in two steps! Use the algebraic formula:
   1. Same Directions -> SUBTRACT (-):
      * "x-th to the LEFT of y-th from LEFT end"  = (y - x)th from LEFT
      * "x-th to the RIGHT of y-th from RIGHT end" = (y - x)th from RIGHT
   2. Opposite Directions -> ADD (+):
      * "x-th to the RIGHT of y-th from LEFT end"  = (y + x)th from LEFT
      * "x-th to the LEFT of y-th from RIGHT end"  = (y + x)th from RIGHT

B) Preceded by vs Followed by (આગળ અને પાછળની ચોખ્ખી ઓળખ):
   - "Preceded by" (પહેલાં / આગળ): Comes BEFORE the element!
   - "Followed by" (પછી / પાછળ): Comes AFTER the element!
   - Formula:
     [ PRECEDING ELEMENT ]  ──►  [ TARGET ]  ──►  [ FOLLOWING ELEMENT ]
     Example: "Vowels preceded by a number and followed by a symbol":
              [ Number ] [ Vowel ] [ Symbol ]

C) Dropping Rules (ઘટકો કાઢી નાખવાનો નિયમ):
   - If asked: "If all numbers are dropped, which element is 8th from right end?"
   - Speed Hack: NEVER cross out numbers on scratch paper!
     Directly count from right end, skipping all digits with your finger.

D) Position Values & Pair Counting:
   - Forward Anchors: EJOTY (E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25)
   - Backward Position: (27 - Forward Position)
   - Pair Counting: Match alphabetical gap forward and backward.
