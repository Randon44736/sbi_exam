1. CORE METHOD: THE OPEN-GATE METHOD (ખુલ્લા દરવાજાની પદ્ધતિ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A) How the Gate Works:
   - '>' : Gate is OPEN from Left to Right. You can travel A -> B.
           Gate is CLOSED from Right to Left. You CANNOT travel B -> A.
   - '<' : Gate is OPEN from Right to Left. You can travel B -> A.
           Gate is CLOSED from Left to Right. You CANNOT travel A -> B.
   - '=' : Gate is OPEN from BOTH sides! You can travel freely A <-> B.
   - '≥' or '≤' : Same as '>' or '<', but carries an 'equal' mat at the bottom.

B) Opposing Gates (Path Blocked / No Relation):
   - Whenever you see opposing signs facing each other:
     Example: A > B < C  OR  A < B > C
     The gate is BLOCKED between A and C!
     You cannot travel from A to C, and you cannot travel from C to A.
     Any direct conclusion like A > C or A < C is immediately FALSE!

C) The 3-Tier Hierarchy (The Kingdom Rule):
   1. King (Priority 1): '>' or '<'
      - Rule for 'A > B' to be TRUE:
        * Path must be open all the way from A to B.
        * At least ONE King ('>') must appear along the path!
   2. Soldier (Priority 2): '≥' or '≤'
      - Rule for 'A ≥ B' to be TRUE:
        * Path must be open all the way from A to B.
        * The 'equal' sign must NEVER drop! Every single gate must have '≥' or '='!
        * If even ONE King ('>') appears without equal, 'A ≥ B' becomes FALSE!
   3. Citizen (Priority 3): '='
      - Rule for 'A = B' to be TRUE:
        * Every single step between A and B must strictly be '='!

D) The Two 'Either-Or' Rules:
   - Case 1 (Combined Breakdown):
     Statement gives: A ≥ B
     Conclusions: I. A > B,  II. A = B
     Both individual conclusions are doubtful, but together they cover A ≥ B.
     -> Answer: Either I or II follows!
   - Case 2 (Blocked Path / Opposing Signs):
     Path between A and B is blocked (e.g., A > M < B).
     All 3 signs must be present: (A > B, A < B, A = B).
     Example: I. A ≥ B,  II. A < B
     -> Answer: Either I or II follows!
