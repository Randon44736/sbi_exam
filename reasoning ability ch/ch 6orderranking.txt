1. CORE METHOD & GOLDEN SPEED FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A) The "Total + 1" Opposite Rank Law (વિરુદ્ધ છેડેથી ક્રમ શોધવાનો નિયમ):
   • When finding rank from opposite end:
     Opposite Rank = (Total + 1) - Given Rank
   • Why (+ 1)? Because the target person must be counted from both directions!
   • Example: Total = 40, Left = 14.
     Right Rank = (40 + 1) - 14 = 41 - 14 = 27th!

B) Total Persons in a Single Row (કુલ સંખ્યા શોધવાનું સૂત્ર):
   • When the rank of ONE person is given from both sides:
     Total = (Left + Right) - 1
   • Why (- 1)? Because the same person was counted twice (from Left and from Right)!

C) Finding Persons "In Between" (વચ્ચે રહેલા લોકો શોધવાના બે કેસ):
   Always check sum of ranks (L + R) against Total first:
   1. Case 1: Simple / Non-Overlapping (સામાન્ય કેસ):
      When (Left + Right) < Total:
      Between = Total - (Left + Right)
   2. Case 2: Overlapping (ઓવરલેપિંગ કેસ - ક્રોસિંગ):
      When (Left + Right) > Total:
      Between = (Left + Right) - Total - 2
      (Why - 2? Because the two reference people are counted twice!).

D) Position Interchange Hack (સ્થાન અદલાબદલીનો શોર્ટકટ):
   • Total = (New rank of Person A) + (Old rank of Person B) - 1.
