MODULE 1: INEQUALITIES (પ્રકરણ ૧: અસમાનતાઓ — OPEN-GATE & SYMBOLS) 3–5 Marks | Target: 1.5 Mins
TRICK 1.1: THE OPEN-GATE PRIORITY LAW (King >, Soldier ≥, Peon =) Open-Gate Walk
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Single or chained inequality statements (e.g. P > Q ≥ R = S < T) with conclusions to verify in 10 seconds.
English Rule: The gate opens from the wide side to the pointed side: A > B means you can walk from A to B; path is closed from B to A.
• King (>, <): To prove A > B true, path from A → B must be open AND have at least ONE King (>).
• Soldier (≥, ≤): To prove A ≥ B true, path must be open AND every single gate must be Soldier (≥) or Peon (=). If even ONE King appears, A ≥ B is FALSE!
• Peon (=): Gate is open both ways.
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: રાજા (>) સાચો કરવા રસ્તામાં ઓછામાં ઓછો એક રાજા જોઈએ. સૈનિક (≥) સાચો કરવા દરેક દરવાજે સૈનિક કે પટાવાળો હોવો જોઈએ; એક પણ રાજા આવે તો ≥ ખોટું પડે!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Statement: P > Q ≥ R = S < T ≤ U
Conclusions: I. P > S  |  II. P ≥ S  |  III. Q > T
[Speed Solution]:
• Conclusion I (P > S): Path P → Q → R → S is completely open, King (>) present → 100% TRUE!
• Conclusion II (P ≥ S): King (>) exists on path, so ≥ cannot hold → FALSE!
• Conclusion III (Q > T): Gate between S and T is closed (S < T opposing) → FALSE! (Only I follows).
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: If opposing signs (> and <) face each other anywhere on the path, the path is blocked → no definite conclusion can be drawn! (સામા-સામા દરવાજા આવે તો રસ્તો બ્લોક).
TRICK 1.2: REVERSE PATH CHAINING (જમણેથી ડાબે મુસાફરી) Right-to-Left
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: When conclusion asks about an element on the right compared to an element on the left (e.g. statement is Z < Y ≤ X = W, conclusion asks W ≥ Y).
English Rule: When walking from right to left, invert your visual perspective: the open mouth must face you as you walk! (e.g., M ≤ N means walking N → M is OPEN with Soldier ≥).
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: જમણેથી ડાબે ચાલતી વખતે ચિહ્ન ઊલટું જોવું: ખુલ્લું મોં તમારી સામે હોવું જોઈએ.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Statement: Z < Y ≤ X = W
Conclusion: W ≥ Y
[Speed Solution]: Walk right-to-left from W → Y: W = X ≥ Y. Gate is open with Soldier/Peon throughout → 100% DEFINITELY TRUE!
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: Seeing '≤' and reflexively assuming the gate is closed, forgetting you are walking from right to left!
TRICK 1.3: THE EITHER-OR SPLITTING LAW (A ≥ B → {A > B} OR {A = B}) Option C Hack
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Both conclusions are individually false, both have identical elements, and the statement proves a Soldier relation (A ≥ B).
English Rule: A definite Soldier relationship A ≥ B means A is either strictly greater than B OR equal to B. Conclusion I (A > B) and Conclusion II (A = B) are individually false, but together form EITHER-OR [Option C]!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: જો વિધાન પરથી સંબંધ A ≥ B બનતો હોય, તો {A > B} અથવા {A = B} બંને એકલા ખોટા છે પણ સાથે મળીને Either-Or [C] બનાવે છે!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Statements: K ≥ L = M ≥ N
Conclusions: I. K > N  |  II. K = N
[Speed Solution]: Path K → N gives K ≥ N (no King). Conclusion I is not definite alone; II is not definite alone. Combined: Either I or II follows [Option C]!
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: Marking Conclusion I as definitely true, forgetting that King is absent on the path!
TRICK 1.4: BLOCKED-PATH EITHER-OR (3 Signs Requirement) 3-Signs Rule
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: When the path between elements is blocked by opposing signs (> <), but conclusions share identical elements.
English Rule: If the path is blocked, relation is unknown. To form Either-Or, ALL THREE signs (>, <, and =) must be present across both conclusions!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: જો રસ્તો બ્લોક હોય, તો Either-Or બનવા માટે ત્રણેય ચિહ્નો (>, <, =) હાજર હોવા જોઈએ. જો માત્ર ૨ ચિહ્નો હોય તો Neither-Nor આવે!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Statement: A > B < C
Conclusions: I. A ≥ C  |  II. A < C
[Speed Solution]: Path A to C is blocked (A > B < C). Conclusion I gives {>, =}; Conclusion II gives {<}. All 3 signs covered → Either I or II follows [Option C]!
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: If conclusions were A > C and A = C (only 2 signs), mark Neither-Nor [Option D], NOT Either-Or!
MODULE 2: SYLLOGISMS (પ્રકરણ ૨: સિલોગિઝમ્સ — ONLY A FEW & POSSIBILITIES) 4–5 Marks | Target: 2.0 Mins
TRICK 2.1: "ONLY A FEW" DUAL TRUTH & POSSIBILITY INVERSION Only a Few
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Statements starting with "Only a few A are B".
English Rule: "Only a few A are B" means TWO things simultaneously:
1. Some A are B (Definite True)  |  2. Some A are NOT B (Definite True).
• The Possibility Inversion Law:
- "All A being B is a possibility" → ALWAYS 100% FALSE (IMPOSSIBLE)!
- "All B being A is a possibility" → ALWAYS 100% TRUE (POSSIBLE)!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: 'Only a few A are B' માં કેટલાક A ચોક્કસ B નથી. તેથી All A in B ક્યારેય શક્ય નથી (0%). પણ All B in A હંમેશાં શક્ય છે (100%)!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Statements: Only a few Rivers are Seas. All Seas are Oceans.
Conclusions: I. All Rivers can be Seas.  |  II. All Seas can be Rivers.
[Speed Solution]:
• Conclusion I (All Rivers can be Seas): In "Only a few", All A in B is impossible → 100% FALSE!
• Conclusion II (All Seas can be Rivers): Reverse possibility is completely valid → 100% TRUE! (Only II follows).
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: Treating "Only a few" like basic "Some", which erroneously allows "All A can be B".
TRICK 2.2: "CAN NEVER BE" = DEFINITELY NOT RULE Definite Negative
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Conclusions phrased as "Some A can never be B".
English Rule: "Can never be" is NOT a possibility; it is a Definite Negative Conclusion (= Some A are definitely not B).
• The All-Positive Premise Law: If all given statements are POSITIVE (All / Some), then "Can never be" is ALWAYS 100% FALSE! (No negative boundaries exist).
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: 'Can never be' સંભાવના નથી પણ ચોક્કસ નકારાત્મક સત્ય છે. જો બધા વિધાનો હકારાત્મક હોય તો 'Can never be' હંમેશાં ખોટું જ પડે!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Statements: All Apples are Fruits. Some Fruits are Sweet.
Conclusion: Some Apples can never be Sweet.
[Speed Solution]: All statements are positive. No negative restriction exists → Conclusion is 100% FALSE!
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: Confusing "can never be" with a possibility statement and assuming it might be true.
TRICK 2.3: THE TRAPPED OVERLAP RULE Trapped Overlap
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Chain where Some A are B, All B are inside C, and No C is D.
English Rule: The portion of A overlapping with B is trapped inside C. Since No C can touch D, that trapped portion of A can NEVER touch D → "Some A are not D" is 100% DEFINITELY TRUE!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: A નો જે ભાગ B માં છે તે C ની અંદર કેદ છે. C ક્યારેય D ને ન અડે, એટલે A નો તે કેદ થયેલો ભાગ પણ ક્યારેય D ન બને → 'Some A are not D' ૧૦૦% સાચું!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Statements: Some Pens are Pencils. All Pencils are Erasers. No Eraser is Marker.
Conclusion: Some Pens are not Markers.
[Speed Solution]: Pens overlapping with Pencils are trapped in Erasers, which cannot touch Markers → 100% DEFINITELY TRUE!
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: Looking for a direct line between Pen and Marker, not finding one, and wrongly marking False!
TRICK 2.4: THE '0 OR >0' EITHER-OR LAW (Some + No) Either-Or Syllogisms
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Both conclusions individually false with identical subject & predicate.
English Rule: If both conclusions are false and the pair is `SOME` + `NO`, overlap between the two sets can only be either 0 (No) or >0 (Some). One must be true → EITHER-OR [Option C]!
⚠️ CRITICAL: `ALL` + `NO` is NEVER EITHER-OR! (Both can be false simultaneously; mark Neither-Nor [D]).
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: SOME + NO ની જોડી હંમેશાં Either-Or [C] બનાવે છે. પરંતુ ALL + NO ક્યારેય Either-Or નથી બનતું (Neither-Nor [D] આવે).
📝 Real Exam Example:
Statements: Some Cats are Dogs. No Dog is Lion.
Conclusions: I. Some Cats are Lions.  |  II. No Cat is Lion.
[Speed Solution]: No direct relation between Cats and Lions. I is false alone, II is false alone. Pair is Some + No → EITHER-OR [Option C]!
MODULE 3: ALPHANUMERIC SERIES & CODING-DECODING (પ્રકરણ ૩, ૪ & ૧૪) 7–8 Marks | Target: 2.5 Mins
TRICK 3.1: DIRECTION ARITHMETIC (Same = Subtract, Opposite = Add) Direction Math
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: "Which element is Nth to the right/left of Mth from the left/right end?"
English Rule:
• Same Directions (L to L or R to R): SUBTRACT ranks → |M - N| from specified end.
• Opposite Directions (R to L or L to R): ADD ranks → M + N from specified end.
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: સરખી દિશા હોય તો બાદબાકી (L-L, R-R), વિરુદ્ધ દિશા હોય તો સરવાળો (L+R, R+L). છેલ્લે પૂછાયેલ છેડેથી ગણતરી કરવી.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Series: A 7 @ B 9 # C 2 $ D 5 & E 8 K ...
Question: Which element is 4th to the right of 9th from the left end?
[Speed Solution]: Opposite directions (Right of Left) → 9 + 4 = 13th from Left end. Count straight to 13 in 3 seconds!
TRICK 3.2: PRECEDED BY VS FOLLOWED BY FRAMEWORK Preceded/Followed
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: "How many numbers are preceded by a symbol and followed by a letter?"
English Rule:
• `Preceded by X` = X comes BEFORE target → [X] → [TARGET]
• `Followed by Y` = Y comes AFTER target → [TARGET] → [Y]
• Unified Search Equation: $\mathbf{[X] \to [TARGET] \to [Y]}$
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: Preceded એટલે આગળ/પહેલાં; Followed એટલે પછી/પાછળ.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Question: How many consonants are preceded by a number and followed by a symbol?
Target Equation: $\mathbf{[\text{Number}] \to [\text{Consonant}] \to [\text{Symbol}]}$
In series: "... 7 B @ ..." → Match found!
TRICK 4.1: CHINESE CODING 2-LINE COMMON ELIMINATION Chinese Coding
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: 3-4 sentence coding blocks asking for the code of a specific word.
English Rule: Never write the entire matrix on paper! Locate the target word, find the TWO lines containing it, and pick the single common code between those lines.
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: રફ પેપરમાં આખી લાઈનો લખવી નહીં. માત્ર પૂછાયેલો શબ્દ કઈ બે લાઈનમાં કોમન છે તે જોઈને સીધો કોડ ઉડાડો.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Line 1: "bank exam score high" = "la ka ta pa"
Line 2: "bank rank easy clear" = "ka ma da za"
Question: What is code for "bank"?
[Speed Solution]: "bank" is only common to Line 1 & 2. Common code is "ka" → Bank = "ka" (solved in 2 secs!).
TRICK 4.2: OPPOSITE LETTER SUM-27 FORMULA (New Pattern Coding) Sum 27 Rule
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: New pattern alphanumeric codes with symbols (e.g. #5V, @6W).
English Rule: The code letter is almost always the Opposite Letter (Sum = 27) of the word's last (or first) letter!
• Formula: $\mathbf{\text{Opposite Rank} = 27 - \text{Letter Rank}}$
• Memorize Pairs: A-Z (1+26), B-Y (2+25), C-X (3+24), D-W (4+23), E-V (5+22), F-U (6+21), G-T (7+20), H-S (8+19), I-R (9+18), J-Q (10+17), K-P (11+16), L-O (12+15), M-N (13+14).
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: કોડ લેટર હંમેશાં છેલ્લા અક્ષરનો સામેનો અક્ષર (સરવાળો ૨૭) હોય છે.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Word = "MOUSE". Last letter = E (5). Opposite Rank = $27 - 5 = 22$ (V) → Code letter is V (e.g. #5V).
If code is #5V → Word MUST end in E ($27 - 22 = 5$)!
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: Picking a word ending in C(3) for code letter V(22), forgetting that $27 - 22 = 5$ (E, not C)!
MODULE 4: BLOOD RELATIONS & DIRECTION SENSE (પ્રકરણ ૫: લોહીના સંબંધો અને દિશા-અંતર) 5–6 Marks | Target: 2.5 Mins
TRICK 5.1: GENERATION LEVEL STACKING & LEVEL GAPS Family Tree
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: 3-generation family tree paragraphs with 7-8 members and married couples.
English Rule:
• Same Horizontal Line (Level 0): Siblings (`A — B`), Married Couples (`A = B`).
• Vertical Downward (Level -1): Children (`↓`).  |  Vertical Upward (Level +1): Parents (`↑`).
• Level Gaps: 0 Gap = Siblings/Couples/Cousins; 1 Gap = Parents/Uncle/Aunt/In-laws; 2 Gaps = Grandparents.
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: ભાઈ-બહેન અને પતિ-પત્ની એક જ આડી લાઈનમાં આવે. સંતાન નીચે અને માતા-પિતા ઉપર.
📝 Real Exam Example:
K is father of L, L is husband of M, M is mother of O.
Level: K (+1) → L, M (0) → O (-1). Gap between K and O is 2 levels → K is Paternal Grandfather of O!
TRICK 5.2: IN-LAW VS GRAND & GENDER ASSUMPTION TRAP Gender Trap
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Questions asking "How is P related to Q?" where gender is not explicitly stated.
English Rule:
• `-IN-LAW` (સાસરિયાં): Exists ONLY via marriage (Spouse's parents/siblings).
• `GRAND-` (દાદા/નાના): Direct 2-generation blood leap. Mother's father is Maternal Grandfather (નાના), NOT father-in-law!
• ⚠️ Gender Rule: NEVER assume gender based on a name (e.g. 'Kiran', 'P'). Gender must be confirmed by relationship terms (brother/mother/son) or pronouns. If unknown → mark "Cannot be determined" or "Son or Daughter".
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: નામ પરથી જાતિ નક્કી ન કરવી. -In-Law માત્ર લગ્નથી બને.
📝 Real Exam Example:
P is child of Q. Q is husband of R. How is P related to R?
P's gender is unknown → Answer: Son or Daughter (or Cannot be determined)!
TRICK 5.3: PYTHAGORAS TRIPLETS & SHORTEST DISTANCE Pythagoras
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Shortest distance between starting point and destination.
English Rule: Shortest displacement: $\mathbf{D = \sqrt{(\Delta X)^2 + (\Delta Y)^2}}$.
• Memorize Instant Triplets: (3, 4 → 5), (6, 8 → 10), (5, 12 → 13), (8, 15 → 17).
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: પાયથાગોરસ ત્રિપુટીઓ સીધી યાદ રાખો: ૬ અને ૮ નો કર્ણ ૧૦ થાય; ૫ અને ૧૨ નો કર્ણ ૧૩ થાય.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Person walks 12m North, turns right and walks 5m East. Shortest distance from start?
$\sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{144 + 25} = \sqrt{169} = \mathbf{13m}$ (Direction: North-East).
TRICK 5.4: SUNRISE/SUNSET SHADOW & FACE-TO-FACE INVERSION Shadow Inversion
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Two friends talking face-to-face in the morning or evening with shadow clues.
English Rule:
• Morning (Sunrise in East → Shadow falls WEST): Facing North → Shadow on LEFT; Facing South → Shadow on RIGHT.
• Face-to-Face Rule: One friend faces North, the other faces South! Calculate the given person's direction, then INVERT for the opposite friend!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: સવારે પડછાયો પશ્ચિમમાં પડે. જો ડાબે પડે તો મુખ ઉત્તર. સામસામે ઉભેલા મિત્રોમાં બીજા મિત્રની દિશા ઊંધી (Opposite) થાય!
📝 Real Exam Example:
One morning, Mohit's shadow falls to his own left. Which direction is Sumit facing if they talk face-to-face?
Mohit's shadow on left in morning → Mohit faces North. Sumit is opposite → Sumit faces SOUTH [Correct Answer]!
MODULE 5: ORDER, RANKING & MACHINE INPUT-OUTPUT (પ્રકરણ ૬ & ૯) 5–8 Marks | Target: 2.5 Mins
TRICK 6.1: SINGLE PERSON TOTAL & POSITION FORMULA T = L + R - 1
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: When a person's rank from left/top and right/bottom is given.
English Rule:
• Total Persons (T): $\mathbf{T = L + R - 1}$
• Finding Position: $\mathbf{R = T - L + 1}$  |  $\mathbf{L = T - R + 1}$
(Why -1? Because that single person is counted twice: once from left, once from right).
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: કુલ સંખ્યા = ડાબેથી ક્રમ + જમણેથી ક્રમ - ૧.
📝 Real Exam Example:
In a row of 40 students, Rahul is 15th from the left. What is his rank from the right end?
$R = 40 - 15 + 1 = 25 + 1 = \mathbf{26th\ from\ right}$!
TRICK 6.2: OVERLAPPING VS NON-OVERLAPPING MIDDLE PERSONS Overlapping Case
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Finding number of persons sitting between Person A (rank L) and Person B (rank R).
English Rule:
• Case 1 (Non-Overlapping: when $T > L + R$): $\mathbf{M = T - (L + R)}$
• Case 2 (Overlapping: when $L + R > T$): $\mathbf{M = (L + R) - T - 2}$
(Why -2? Because Person A and Person B cross each other and are double-counted).
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: જો $L + R > T$ થાય તો ઓવરલેપિંગ કહેવાય. વચ્ચેની સંખ્યા = $(L + R) - T - 2$.
📝 Real Exam Example:
In a row of 50 persons, A is 30th from left and B is 25th from right. How many between them?
$30 + 25 = 55 > 50$ (Overlap!). $M = 55 - 50 - 2 = 5 - 2 = \mathbf{3\ persons}$!
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: Forgetting the -2 in overlapping cases and wrongly answering 5!
TRICK 9.1: PEN-FREE MACHINE INPUT-OUTPUT LABELING Pen-Free I/O
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Shifting word/number machines.
English Rule: Do not rewrite the entire line for each step! Label elements directly in the input row:
• Left-shifted: $L_1, L_2, L_3 \dots$  |  Right-shifted: $R_1, R_2, R_3 \dots$
• Auto-Arrange Rule: If an element is already in place when its turn arrives, it takes 0 steps!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: આખી લાઈન વારંવાર ન લખવી. મૂળ ઇનપુટમાં જ $L_1, L_2$ નંબર આપી દો. ઓટો-અરેન્જ થયેલ તત્વ ૦ સ્ટેપ લે છે.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Words move Left alphabetically, Numbers move Right descending. If highest number is already at the extreme right in the input, it is automatically arranged in Step 1 with 0 extra steps!
TRICK 9.2: THE STEP INVERSION BACKWARD LAW (Cannot Be Determined) CND in 2 Secs
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: "If Step 3 is given, what was the Input or Step 1?"
English Rule: In ANY machine input-output (shifting or box), YOU CAN NEVER GO BACKWARD FROM A STEP TO INPUT OR PREVIOUS STEP!
• Answer: Always mark "Cannot be determined" (નક્કી ન થઈ શકે) immediately in 2 seconds!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: કોઈપણ સ્ટેપ પરથી પાછળ (ઇનપુટ તરફ) જઈ શકાતું નથી. સીધો જવાબ 'Cannot be determined' આવે છે.
📝 Real Exam Example:
"If Step IV of an input is: apple 92 cat 85 dog 41, what will be Step II?" → Answer: Cannot be determined!
MODULE 6: SEATING & PUZZLES CORE (પ્રકરણ ૭ & ૮: બેઠક વ્યવસ્થા અને પઝલ્સ મૂળભૂત) 15–20 Marks | Target: 7.0 Mins
TRICK 7.1: CIRCULAR/SQUARE FACING COMPASS (Inside vs Outside) Seating Compass
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Circular or Square tables with persons facing center or outside.
English Rule:
• Facing Inside (Center): Right = Counter-Clockwise (CCW); Left = Clockwise (CW).
• Facing Outside (Away): Right = Clockwise (CW); Left = Counter-Clockwise (CCW).
• North Facing Anchor: A person facing North (Top edge facing outside $\uparrow$) has Right = East (Clockwise)! Never invert when someone faces North!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: અંદર જોતાં હોય તો Right = વિરુદ્ધ ઘડિયાળ (CCW). બહાર જોતાં હોય તો Right = ઘડિયાળની દિશા (Clockwise).
📝 Real Exam Example:
8 persons sit around a circle facing outside. Q sits 2nd to the right of P.
P faces outside → Move 2 steps Clockwise from P → Q's seat is fixed!
TRICK 7.2: THE 2-PARALLEL CASES GOLDEN LAW 2 Parallel Cases
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Any puzzle/seating with dual possibilities in the first 2 clues.
English Rule: Never hold possibilities mentally. Draw Case 1 and Case 2 side-by-side immediately. Subsequent clues will contradict and eliminate one case, leaving the correct arrangement 100% solved in ≤ 2.5 minutes.
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: મનમાં વિચારવાને બદલે રફ પેપર પર Case 1 અને Case 2 સમાંતર દોરી નાખો. આગળ જતાં ૧ શરત એક કેસને ઉડાડી દેશે!
📝 Real Exam Example:
"P sits at an extreme end of the row."
Instantly draw: Case 1 (P at extreme left end)  |  Case 2 (P at extreme right end).
TRICK 8.1: FLOOR PUZZLE SETUP & GAP ARITHMETIC Floor Setup
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: 7-8 storey building puzzles.
English Rule: Write vertical floor numbers 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 (Top floor at top, Floor 1 at bottom).
• "N persons live between A and B" → $\mathbf{|\text{Floor}_A - \text{Floor}_B| = N + 1}$ (3 persons between means floor difference is 4!).
• "A lives immediately above B" → $\text{Floor}_A = \text{Floor}_B + 1$.
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: ઊભા માળ ૭ થી ૧ લખવા. વચ્ચે ૩ વ્યક્તિઓ હોય તો માળનો તફાવત ૪ થાય ($3 + 1 = 4$).
📝 Real Exam Example:
A lives on Floor 6. Three persons live between A and B. B lives below A.
Floor of B = $6 - (3 + 1) = 6 - 4 = \mathbf{Floor\ 2}$! (Floors 3, 4, 5 are between them).
TRICK 8.2: MONTH-DATE 30 VS 31 DAY ANCHORING Month-Date
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: 4 months (e.g. March, April, May, June) with 2 dates (e.g. 7th and 20th).
English Rule: Write the number of days next to each month immediately: March (31), April (30), May (31), June (30).
• Clue: "P was born in a month having 30 days on an odd date" → Instantly limits P to only April 7 or June 7 (just 2 cases!).
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: મહિનાના દિવસો (૩૦ કે ૩૧) લખી લો. 'Month having 30 days' કહે તો તરત જ ૩૦ વાળા મહિનાની તારીખ પકડો.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Months: April (30), May (31). Dates: 7, 20. "P born in 30-day month on odd date" → P = April 7!
MODULE 7: DATA SUFFICIENCY & MISCELLANEOUS REASONING (પ્રકરણ ૧૦ & ૧૧) 5–8 Marks | Target: 2.5 Mins
TRICK 10.1: DATA SUFFICIENCY 2-STATEMENT ELIMINATION TREE DS Elimination
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Questions with Statement I and Statement II to check sufficiency.
English Rule:
1. Test Statement I alone → If sufficient, note it.
2. Test Statement II alone → If sufficient and I was also sufficient → [D] (Either I alone or II alone)!
3. Test (I + II) together ONLY if both failed individually → If combined they give a unique answer → [E] (Both together), else [C] (Neither).
• Stop-Solving Law: Do not calculate final numerical value; stop the moment uniqueness is proven!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: આખો દાખલો સોલ્વ કરવાનો નથી. માત્ર એટલું જ નક્કી કરો કે ૧ ચોક્કસ જવાબ મળે છે કે નહીં!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Who is tallest among A, B, C?
Statement I: A is taller than B and C. → I alone is sufficient (A is tallest).
Statement II: B is the shortest. → II alone is not sufficient (A or C unknown). → Answer: Statement I alone is sufficient!
TRICK 10.2: DEFINITE NEGATIVE ('NO') IS 100% SUFFICIENT! Definite NO
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Yes/No Data Sufficiency questions (e.g. "Is P facing North?").
English Rule: Data is SUFFICIENT if it yields a definite YES OR a definite NO! A definitive negative answer is 100% sufficient.
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: જો વિધાન સાબિત કરે કે 'ના, P દક્ષિણ જોવે છે' → તો પણ ડેટા ૧૦૦% પર્યાપ્ત છે! કારણ કે ચોક્કસ 'ના' જવાબ મળી ગયો.
📝 Real Exam Example:
Question: Is P facing North? Statement says: P faces South.
Definite answer is NO → Data is 100% SUFFICIENT!
TRICK 11.1: ALPHABET NUMERICAL TRANSLATION FOR LETTER PAIRS Letter Pairs
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: "How many pairs of letters in 'CONTAINER' have as many letters between them as in the alphabet..."
English Rule: Do NOT recite letters mentally! Write numerical ranks above letters: A=1, B=2, ..., Z=26.
• Recite numbers sequentially forward and backward (e.g. from 16 recite 17, 18, 19...). When spoken number matches written number → instant pair!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: અક્ષરોની ઉપર અંક લખી દો (A=1...Z=26). ૧૬ પછી ૧૭, ૧૮, ૧૯ બોલો; જે બોલો તે જ અંક સામે દેખાય એટલે જોડી રોકડી!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Word: POST → Numbers: 16, 15, 19, 20.
Forward: From 19 recite "20" → matches 20 → Pair (S, T)!
Backward: From 15 recite "16" → matches 16 → Pair (O, P)!
TRICK 11.2: MEANINGFUL WORD 15-SECOND SKIP RULE 1-X-Y Rule
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Forming meaningful words from specified letters (mark X if more than one, Y if none).
English Rule: English vocabulary is vast. If a common word doesn't pop up within 15 seconds, SKIP IT IMMEDIATELY and mark for review in minute 24! Avoid negative marking on obscure 4-letter anagrams.
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: જો ૧૫ સેકન્ડમાં સામાન્ય શબ્દ ન દેખાય, તો આ પ્રશ્ન તરત છોડી દો (Skip). નેગેટિવ માર્કિંગ (-0.25) થી બચવાનો આ બેસ્ટ રસ્તો છે!
📝 Real Exam Example:
Letters: E, A, T, M → TEAM, MEAT, MATE, TAME (4 words exist!). Easy to miss 'TAME' and lose marks. Skip if not 100% certain!

TRICK 12.1: FLOOR + FLAT 4X2 MATRIX ARCHITECTURE Floor + Flat
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: 8 persons living on 4 floors with Flat P (West) and Flat Q (East).
English Rule:
• Grid Setup: Floors 4, 3, 2, 1 vertically. Column 1 = Flat P (West), Column 2 = Flat Q (East).
• "A lives immediately above B in the same flat" → Same column, adjacent floor.
• "A lives immediately above B" (flat not specified) → Can be same flat OR different flat!
• "A lives North-East of B" → B is on lower floor in Flat P; A is on upper floor in Flat Q!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: રફ પેપરમાં ઊભા માળ ૪, ૩, ૨, ૧ અને બે કોલમ Flat P અને Flat Q દોરો.
📝 Real Exam Example:
B lives on an even floor in Flat P. C lives East of B.
Even floors = 4 or 2. Candidate slots: (Floor 4: P=B, Q=C) OR (Floor 2: P=B, Q=C).
TRICK 12.2: DESIGNATION HIERARCHY FIXED BACKBONE Designation
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: 7-8 executive ranks: Chairman > GM > DGM > AGM > Manager > Officer > Clerk.
English Rule: ALWAYS fix senior-to-junior vertical backbone first: `1: Chairman > 2: GM > 3: DGM > 4: AGM > 5: Manager > 6: Officer > 7: Clerk`.
• "R is senior to only two persons" → Senior to Officer(6) & Clerk(7) → R is Manager (Rank 5)!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: હંમેશાં સિનિયરથી જુનિયર ઊભો ક્રમ અગાઉથી લખી લો. વ્યક્તિઓ આ કરોડરજ્જુ પર ઝડપથી બેસી જશે.

TRICK 13.1: TOP-EDGE OUTSIDE COMPASS (Ch 13 Hard Topic Fix) Top-Edge Compass
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: Square/Rectangular table where middle-side persons face outside.
English Rule: A person sitting at the TOP edge facing OUTSIDE (↑) is facing North!
• Physical Compass Law: Facing North means YOUR Right is East (Clockwise)! Do NOT invert direction when someone faces North!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: ટેબલની ઉપરની ધાર (Top Middle) પર બેઠેલી વ્યક્તિ બહાર જોતી હોય તો ઉત્તર જોવે છે → Right = પૂર્વ (East/Clockwise). દિશા ઊલટી ન કરવી!
📝 Real Exam Example:
U sits at Top Middle facing outside (↑). What is 2nd to right of U?
Facing North → Right is Clockwise (East) → Move 2 steps clockwise → Right-edge seat!
⚠️ Exam Trap / ચેતવણી: Inverting right to left for a top-edge person who faces outside (this caused the error in Ch 13 practice!).
TRICK 13.2: STEP-ZERO LAW & TRIANGULAR ALTERNATE PARITY Triangular Parity
🎯 How to Identify in Exam / પરીક્ષામાં ઓળખ: 6-person triangular table (3 corners, 3 side middles) or distance counting.
English Rule:
• Step-Zero Law: Reference person is Step 0. Step 1 = Immediate neighbor; Step 2 = Second to right/left (1 person between).
• Triangular Alternate Parity: Corners and Side middles strictly alternate: $\text{Corner} \to \text{Side} \to \text{Corner} \to \text{Side}$.
- 1 step from Corner = Side Middle.
- 2 steps from Corner = MUST BE ANOTHER CORNER!
ગુજરાતી નિયમ: ત્રિકોણાકાર ટેબલમાં ખૂણાથી ૨ સ્ટેપ હંમેશાં બીજો ખૂણો જ હોય!
📝 Real Exam Example:
P is at a Corner. Q sits 2nd to the right of P.
2 steps from Corner → Q MUST be at a Corner! (Never place Q at a side middle).
