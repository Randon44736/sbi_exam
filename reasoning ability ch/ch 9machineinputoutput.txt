1. CORE METHOD & SHIFTING MECHANICS (મશીન ઇનપુટ-આઉટપુટ િસદ્ધાંતો)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A. THE 3-SECOND PATTERN IDENTIFICATION RULE:
   • To instantly deduce the machine logic, NEVER look at Steps I, II, III first!
   • DIRECT COMPARISON (Input vs Final Step):
     - Compare the INPUT directly with the FINAL STEP (Step IV).
     - Left end of Step IV: 73  58  27  14  (Numbers in Descending Order)
     - Right end of Step IV: bat  lion  orange  zebra  (Words in Alphabetical Order A-Z)
   • SHIFTING DIRECTION (Inward vs Outward Shifting):
     - Check Step I: 14 was moved to extreme left.
     - Check Step II: 27 was placed OUTSIDE 14 (on the far left: 27 14...).
     - Check Step III: 58 was placed OUTSIDE 27 (on the far left: 58 27 14...).
     - This is OUTWARD LEFT-SHIFTING for Numbers!
     - Similarly for words: 'bat' went to extreme right in Step I.
     - In Step II, 'lion' was placed OUTSIDE 'bat' (...bat lion).
     - In Step III, 'orange' was placed OUTSIDE 'lion' (...bat lion orange).
     - This is OUTWARD RIGHT-SHIFTING for Words!

B. THE ZERO-PEN INDEX NUMBERING METHOD (પરીક્ષામાં સમય બચાવવાની શ્રેષ્ઠ પદ્ધતિ):
   • In banking exams, candidates who rewrite full words 4 times waste 4 minutes!
   • Shortcut:
     - Number the words by their alphabetical rank:
       bat = W1, lion = W2, orange = W3, zebra = W4
     - Number the digits by their target processing order:
       14 = N1, 27 = N2, 58 = N3, 73 = N4
     - Step 1 moves N1 to left, W1 to right.
     - Step 2 moves N2 to left, W2 to right.
     - Unmoved elements retain their original relative order.

C. THE IRREVERSIBILITY LAW (અપરિવર્તનીયતાનો નિયમ):
   • Sorting is a Many-to-One mathematical mapping (40,320 inputs yield the same sorted output!).
   • Therefore: You can NEVER determine the Previous Step or the Original Input from a later step!
   • If an exam question asks: "What was Step I?" given Step III -> Mark "Cannot be determined" in 1 second!

D. THE CONVEYOR BELT INTUITION & VISUAL STEP-BY-STEP BREAKDOWN (દેશી અને સરળ સમજૂતી):
   • Analogy: Imagine a conveyor belt with 4 numbers (58, 27, 14, 73) and 4 words (orange, bat, zebra, lion).
   • In EVERY Step, the machine performs exactly TWO actions:
     1. Left End (ડાબી બાજુ): Pick the SMALLEST remaining number and pull it to the extreme Left.
     2. Right End (જમણી બાજુ): Pick the Alphabetically EARLIEST remaining word (A-Z) and pull it to the extreme Right.
     3. Middle Elements (વચ્ચેના બાકી રહેલા): DO NOT TOUCH THEM! Keep them in their exact same relative order!
   • Visual Transformation from Input to Step I:
     Input:   orange  58  [bat]  27  zebra  [14]  lion  73
     Action:  Pull [14] to far-left, Pull [bat] to far-right.
     Result:  [ 14 ]  +  orange  58  27  zebra  lion  73  +  [ bat ]
     Step I:  14  orange  58  27  zebra  lion  73  bat

E. HOW TO COUNT TOTAL STEPS & THE AUTO-ARRANGEMENT LAW (સ્ટેપ ગણવાનો સુવર્ણ નિયમ):
   • Will there always be 4 steps? NO!
   • Formula for Maximum Steps:
     - Case 1: One-End Shifting (only Left or only Right):
       Max Steps = N - 1 (where N is total elements). The last element auto-aligns.
     - Case 2: Two-End Simultaneous Shifting (1 Left + 1 Right per step):
       Max Steps = Total Elements / 2.
       Here: 8 elements / 2 = 4 Maximum Steps.
   • The Auto-Arrangement Exception (આપોઆપ ગોઠવાઈ જવાનો નિયમ):
     - If an element is ALREADY sitting in its desired sorted position when its turn comes, it does NOT require a separate step! It gets "Auto-Arranged" for free!
     - In that case: Total Steps = Max Steps - Number of Auto-Arranged elements.
     - In this problem: In Step III, neither 73 nor zebra were auto-aligned, so Step IV was mandatory -> exactly 4 steps!

🏆 CHAPTER 9 GOLDEN REVISION SHEET & PRO CHEAT-SHEET
====================================================================

1. PATTERN IDENTIFICATION IN 3 SECONDS:
   • Compare INPUT directly with the FINAL STEP (Skip middle steps!).
   • Identify Left-sorting rule (e.g. Numbers: Descending or Ascending).
   • Identify Right-sorting rule (e.g. Words: Alphabetical A-Z or Reverse Z-A).

2. SHIFTING DYNAMICS:
   • Outward Push: New element is added at the outermost boundary, pushing existing sorted elements inward.
     - Left: Step 1 = [N1 ...], Step 2 = [N2 N1 ...], Step 3 = [N3 N2 N1 ...]
     - Right: Step 1 = [... W1], Step 2 = [... W1 W2], Step 3 = [... W1 W2 W3]
   • Inward Push: Existing elements stay at ends, new elements enter inside.

3. THE STEP-COUNT LAW:
   • 2-End Simultaneous Shifting: Max Steps = Total Elements / 2.
   • 1-End Shifting: Max Steps = Total Elements - 1.
   • Auto-Arrangement: If an element is already in its destination when its turn comes, it takes 0 steps!

4. THE IRREVERSIBILITY AXIOM:
   • Given Step K, you can NEVER determine Step K-1 or Input (Always "Cannot be determined").
   • Given Step K, you CAN determine Step K+1, K+2, or Final Step (Forward simulation is 100% possible).
