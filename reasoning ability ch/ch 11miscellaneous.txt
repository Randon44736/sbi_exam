1. CORE SUB-TOPICS & CONCEPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A) Letter Pair Formation (Forward & Backward):
   - What is the Gap Rule?
     * The gap can be ANY size: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, or more letters!
     * The only condition: The gap in the word MUST EQUAL the gap in the English alphabet.
     * Examples:
       - Gap = 0: (S, T) in POST -> adjacent in alphabet, adjacent in word.
       - Gap = 1: (G, I) in BANKING -> alphabet: G [H] I (1 letter). Word: G [N] I (1 letter).
       - Gap = 3: (P, T) in PRINT -> alphabet: P [Q R S] T (3 letters). Word: P [R I N] T (3 letters).
   - Why the Finger-Recite Method is Magic:
     * Every single letter in the word gets its own turn as the starting point!
     * Left-to-Right: Letter 1 tests rightward, Letter 2 tests rightward, Letter 3 tests rightward...
     * Right-to-Left: Last letter tests leftward, 2nd-last tests leftward...
     * You do NOT need to calculate gaps in your head! When you recite ABCs from letter to letter, your mouth automatically matches the gap! If your mouth says the letter that your finger is pointing at, the gap is automatically 100% identical!
   - Crucial Rule: Do NOT loop from Z back to A unless explicitly instructed.

B) Meaningful Word Formation (The 1-X-Y Trap):
   - Format: "Extract 1st, 3rd, 5th, 8th letters. If one word forms, mark 3rd letter. If more than 1, mark X. If none, mark Y."
   - Exam Tactic: This carries the highest error rate in IBPS RRB PO! 
     If anagrams are not immediately apparent, SKIP and return only in the final 2 minutes.
   - Frequent Bank Anagram Roots:
     * D-E-A-R -> DEAR, DARE, READ
     * P-O-S-T -> POST, SPOT, STOP, TOPS, POTS
     * T-A-M-E -> TAME, MEAT, TEAM, MATE
     * R-A-C-E -> RACE, CARE, ACRE
     * S-E-A-T -> SEAT, EAST, EATS

C) Number & Digit Rearrangement:
   - "Add 1 to odd digits, subtract 1 from even digits; find digits appearing more than once."
   - Shortcut: Never write the full 8-digit new number. Directly scan for which original digits will land on the same result (e.g. odd 3 + 1 = 4; even 5 [not even] or even 6 - 1 = 5).

D) Odd One Out (Letter Clusters):
   - Check difference between consecutive letters:
     Example: CEG (+2, +2), PRT (+2, +2), KMO (+2, +2), TVX (+2, +2), SUW (+2, +2).
   - Variations: Vowel counts, reverse opposite pairs, consonant gaps.

E) Alphabetical & Dictionary Order:
   - Arranging words in alphabetical dictionary sequence.
   - Arranging letters of a word alphabetically from left to right; identifying which remain unchanged.


2. VERIFIED SOLVED EXAMPLES (IBPS RRB PO PREVIOUS-YEAR MODELS)

[Example 1 - Verified RRB PO Prelims]:
Word: 'BANKING'
Letters: B(2), A(1), N(14), K(11), I(9), N(14), G(7)

Step 1: Check Forward (Left to Right):
  - Start at B: say C, D, E, F, G, H -> No match.
  - Start at A: say B, C, D, E, F -> No match.
  - Start at N: say O, P, Q, R -> No match.
  - Start at K: say L, M, N -> No match.
  - Start at I: say J, K -> No match.
  - Start at N: say O -> No match.
Forward pairs = 0.

Step 2: Check Backward (Right to Left):
  - Start at G (last letter):
    * Move to N: say 'H' (no match)
    * Move to I: say 'I' -> MATCH! The letter on paper IS 'I'!
      -> Pair 1: (G, I) [In alphabet: G, H, I. In word: G, N, I. Exactly 1 letter between!]
    * Move to K: say 'J' (no match)
    * Move to N: say 'K' (no match)
    * Move to A: say 'L' (no match)
    * Move to B: say 'M' (no match)
  - Start at N: say O, P, Q, R, S -> No match.
  - Start at I: say J, K, L, M -> No match.
  - Start at K: say L, M, N -> No match.
  - Start at N: say O, P -> No match.
  - Start at A:
    * Move to B: say 'B' -> MATCH! The letter on paper IS 'B'!
      -> Pair 2: (A, B) [In alphabet: A, B are adjacent. In word: B and A are adjacent!]

Total Pairs in 'BANKING' = 2 (G-I and A-B). Answer: [C] Two.
