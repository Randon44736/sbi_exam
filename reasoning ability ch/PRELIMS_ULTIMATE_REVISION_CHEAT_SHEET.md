## 🏛️ SECTION 1: SPEED CORE SHORTCUTS (0 TO 5 MINUTES — 10 MARKS)

### 1. Inequalities (પ્રકરણ ૧: અસમાનતાઓ)
* **The Open-Gate Rule (ખુલ્લા દરવાજાનો નિયમ):**
  * Gate opens from wide side to pointed side: `A > B` means you can walk from $A \to B$. Gate is closed from $B \to A$.
  * Symbols: `>` or `<` (King / રાજા), `≥` or `≤` (Soldier / સૈનિક), `=` (Peon / દરવાજો કાયમ ખુલ્લો).
* **The 3 Definite Truth Rules:**
  1. To prove `A > B`: Path from $A$ to $B$ must be completely open, AND there must be at least ONE King (`>`) on the path!
  2. To prove `A ≥ B`: Path must be open, AND EVERY single gate must be at least Soldier (`≥` or `=`). If even ONE King appears, `A ≥ B` is FALSE!
  3. Opposing Gates (`>` and `<` face each other): Path is blocked $\implies$ 100% FALSE (Can never be definitely true).
* **The Either-Or Rule (C):**
  * Check when both conclusions are false individually with identical elements (e.g. $A$ and $B$).
  * If relation between $A$ and $B$ is $A \ge B$, it splits into: Conclusion 1: $A > B$, Conclusion 2: $A = B \implies$ **EITHER-OR [C]**!
  * If path between $A$ and $B$ is blocked (opposing signs), you need ALL 3 signs ($A > B$, $A < B$, $A = B$) to form Either-Or!

### 2. Syllogisms (પ્રકરણ ૨: સિલોગિઝમ્સ)
* **"Only a Few A are B" (માત્ર અમુક જ A એ B છે):**
  * Means TWO things simultaneously:
    1. **Some A are B (Definite True)**
    2. **Some A are NOT B (Definite True)**
  * **Critical Possibility Trap:**
    * "All A can be B is a possibility" $\implies$ **ALWAYS 100% FALSE!** (Never possible!).
    * "All B can be A is a possibility" $\implies$ **ALWAYS 100% TRUE!**
* **The Trapped Overlap Rule (ટ્રેપ્ડ ઓવરલેપ નિયમ):**
  * If Some $A$ are $B$, and All $B$ are inside $C$, and No $C$ is $D$:
    $\implies$ The portion of $A$ trapped inside $B$ can NEVER touch $D$.
    $\implies$ "Some A are not D" is **100% DEFINITELY TRUE!**
* **The '0 or >0' Either-Or Law (C):**
  * If both conclusions are false individually, elements are identical, and pair is:
    * `SOME` + `NO` (સૌથી કોમન)
    * `ALL` + `SOME-NOT`
    $\implies$ Mark **EITHER-OR [C]**!
  * *Trap:* `ALL` + `NO` is **NEVER EITHER-OR**! (Mark Neither-Nor [D]).

## 🧭 SECTION 2: POSITIONAL & MISCELLANEOUS (5 TO 10 MINUTES — 10 MARKS)

### 3. Alphanumeric Series & Alphabet Test (પ્રકરણ ૩ & ૧૧)
* **Same Direction vs Opposite Direction Rule:**
  * Same directions (Left of Left, or Right of Right) $\implies$ **SUBTRACT** ranks ($L - L$ or $R - R$).
  * Opposite directions (Right of Left, or Left of Right) $\implies$ **ADD** ranks ($L + R$ or $R + L$).
* **Preceded by vs Followed by (આગળ અને પાછળ):**
  * `[Preceded by X] $\to$ [TARGET] $\to$ [Followed by Y]`
  * ગુજરાતી: `[X આગળ / પહેલાં] $\to$ [મુખ્ય તત્વ] $\to$ [Y પાછળ / પછી]`.
* **Letter Pair Counting Shortcut (જોડી ગણતરી):**
  * Never count English letters mentally! Translate directly to numbers ($A=1, B=2, \dots, Z=26$).
  * Forward and Backward: If counting from 16 (P), recite $17, 18, 19\dots$ match gives instant pair!

### 4. Blood Relations (પ્રકરણ ૫: લોહીના સંબંધો)
* **The Generation Level Rule (પેઢી સ્તર નિયમ):**
  * Same Horizontal Line: Siblings (`—`), Married Couple (`=`).
  * Vertical Drop: Children drop down 1 level (`↓`). Parents rise 1 level (`↑`).
  * Level Difference:
    * 0 gap $\implies$ Brother, Sister, Husband, Wife, Cousin.
    * 1 gap $\implies$ Father, Mother, Uncle, Aunt, Father-in-law.
    * 2 gaps $\implies$ Grandfather, Grandmother.
* **The In-Law vs Grand Rule:**
  * `-IN-LAW` (સાસરિયાં) ONLY exists via marriage!
  * `GRAND-` (દાદા/નાના) is a 2-generation blood leap! Mother's father is Maternal Grandfather (નાના), NOT Father-in-law!
* **Name Trap:** Never assume gender from a name (e.g. 'Kiran', 'Suman'). Gender is ONLY fixed by "he/she", "brother/sister", or marital pairing!

### 5. Direction Sense (પ્રકરણ ૫: દિશા અને અંતર)
* **The 4 Cardinal & 4 Intercardinal Directions:**
  * North $\uparrow$, South $\downarrow$, East $\to$, West $\leftarrow$.
  * Turning Right: Clockwise ($90^\circ$ CW).
  * Turning Left: Counter-Clockwise ($90^\circ$ CCW).
* **Pythagoras Theorem:**
  * Shortest Distance: $D = \sqrt{(\Delta X)^2 + (\Delta Y)^2}$.
  * Common Triplets: $(3, 4, 5)$, $(6, 8, 10)$, $(5, 12, 13)$, $(8, 15, 17)$.
* **Shadow Rule (સૂર્ય અને પડછાયો):**
  * Morning (Sunrise in East $\to$ Shadow falls to **WEST**).
    * If shadow is to your Left $\implies$ You face **NORTH**.
    * If shadow is to your Right $\implies$ You face **SOUTH**.
  * Evening (Sunset in West $\to$ Shadow falls to **EAST**).
  * *Face-to-Face Inversion Trap:* In face-to-face 2-friend shadow problems, find Person A's direction, then INVERT for Person B!

## 🧩 SECTION 3: PUZZLES & SEATING MASTERY (10 TO 22 MINUTES — 20 MARKS)

### 6. Seating Arrangements Core & Advanced (પ્રકરણ ૭ & ૧૩)
* **The Compass Orientation Law:**
  * Facing Inside (Center) $\implies$ Right is Counter-Clockwise; Left is Clockwise.
  * Facing Outside (Away from Center) $\implies$ Right is Clockwise; Left is Counter-Clockwise!
  * **Top-Edge Rule:** Facing North/Outside $\uparrow \implies$ YOUR Right is East (Clockwise)! Never invert when facing North!
* **Step Zero Law (અંતર ગણતરી):**
  * Reference person is Step 0.
  * 1 step away = Immediate neighbor (0 persons between).
  * 2 steps away = Second to right/left (1 person between).
  * 3 steps away = Third to right/left (2 persons between).
* **Triangular Table Parity Law (ત્રિકોણાકાર ટેબલ):**
  * In a 6-person triangle (3 corners + 3 side middles), Corners and Sides strictly **ALTERNATE**!
  * 1 step from Corner = Side Middle.
  * 2 steps from Corner = MUST be another Corner!

### 7. Puzzles Core & Advanced (પ્રકરણ ૮ & ૧૨)
* **Floor + Flat Puzzles (4 Floors × 2 Flats: Flat A & B):**
  * Grid Setup: Vertical Floor numbers 4, 3, 2, 1. Two columns: Flat A (West), Flat B (East).
  * "P lives on a floor immediately above Q" $\implies$ Can be same flat or different flat UNLESS stated "in the same flat".
  * "P lives to the North-East of Q" $\implies$ Q is on a lower floor in Flat A, P is on an upper floor in Flat B!
* **Designation Hierarchy Puzzles:**
  * Fixed Order: Always arrange strictly from Senior-most to Junior-most:
    `Director > GM > DGM > AGM > Manager > AM > Clerk`.
  * Create 2 parallel cases side-by-side immediately from the 1st strong constraint.
* **Month-Date Puzzles (4 Months × 2 Dates: e.g. 7th & 20th):**
  * Mark number of days in each month immediately (30 vs 31 days).
  * Clues like "Born in a month having 30 days" instantly narrows candidates down to 2 slots!
