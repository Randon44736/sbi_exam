1. CORE METHOD & GOLDEN SPEED PROTOCOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A) Blood Relation Family Tree Notations (સંબંધ વૃક્ષ સંજ્ઞાઓ):
   • Male (પુરુષ): [+] (Plus or Box)
   • Female (સ્ત્રી): [-] (Minus or Circle)
   • Married Couple (દંપતી): [ = ] (Double horizontal bond, e.g. Husband = Wife)
   • Siblings (ભાઈ-બહેન): [ — ] (Single horizontal dash, e.g. Bro — Sis)
   • Generations (પેઢીઓ): [ | ] (Vertical line: Grandparents -> Parents -> Children)

B) Golden Rules for Blood Relations:
   1. The "Starting from MY" Rule (પોતાનાથી શરૂઆત કરવાનો નિયમ):
      In pointing/dialogue questions ("Pointing to a person..."):
      Always start from the word "MY" (મારી/મારા) and decode backwards to yourself!
   2. The Gender Trap (જાતિ નક્કી કરવાનો નિયમ):
      NEVER assume gender from a name! (e.g. Kiran, Suman, Sonu can be male or female).
      Only assign [+] or [-] when words like 'he', 'she', 'father', 'sister', 'son', etc. appear.
   3. The "TO" vs "OF" Relationship Direction:
      "How is P related TO S?" means: "Who is P with respect to S?" (P કોણ થાય?).
      Do not answer who S is!

C) Direction Sense & Shortest Distance (દિશા અને ટૂંકું અંતર):
   1. The 8 Compass Directions:
      North (ઉત્તર), South (દક્ષિણ), East (પૂર્વ), West (પશ્ચિમ).
      NE (ઉત્તર-પૂર્વ / ઈશાન), NW (ઉત્તર-પશ્ચિમ / વાયવ્ય),
      SE (દક્ષિણ-પૂર્વ / અગ્નિ), SW (દક્ષિણ-પશ્ચિમ / નૈઋત્ય).
   2. Standard Turning Rules:
      • Turning RIGHT = 90° Clockwise (ઘડિયાળના કાંટાની દિશામાં).
      • Turning LEFT = 90° Anticlockwise (ઘડિયાળની વિરુદ્ધ દિશામાં).
   3. Shortest Distance (Pythagoras Triplets):
      Distance = √(Base² + Perpendicular²)
      Memorized Triplets:
      • 3 - 4 - 5
      • 6 - 8 - 10
      • 5 - 12 - 13
      • 8 - 15 - 17
   4. Shadow Laws (પડછાયાના નિયમો):
      • Morning / Sunrise (સૂર્યોદય): Sun is in EAST ──► Shadow falls in WEST!
      • Evening / Sunset (સૂર્યાસ્ત): Sun is in WEST ──► Shadow falls in EAST!
      • Exactly 12 Noon: NO SHADOW!

  Gen 1: D (+)  [Maternal Grandfather / નાના]
          |
  Gen 2: C (-)  [Mother / માતા]
          |
  Gen 3: A (+) --- B  [A is male, Daughter's son = Grandson!]
• Answer is uniquely [C] Grandson!
