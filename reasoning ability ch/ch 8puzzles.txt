1. CORE METHOD & GOLDEN PUZZLE SOLVING PROTOCOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A. FLOOR PUZZLE METHODOLOGY (7 & 8 Floors):
   • Standard Numbering: Ground floor is 1, above is 2, ..., topmost is N. NEVER invert!
   • Gap Distance Formula:
     - "K persons live between A and B" => |Floor_A - Floor_B| = K + 1.
     - "3 persons between A and B" => Gap of 4 floors (|A - B| = 4).
   • Immediate Positions:
     - "A lives immediately above B" => Floor_A = Floor_B + 1.
     - "A lives immediately below B" => Floor_A = Floor_B - 1.
   • The 2-Case Execution Law:
     - Never keep cases in your head. Draw 2 parallel columns on paper immediately.
     - Place first definite constraint in both branches.
     - Strike out dead cases the moment an impossible placement occurs.

B. BOX STACKING METHODOLOGY (7 to 9 Boxes):
   • Dynamic Relative Blocks:
     - Boxes lack fixed ground numbers at the start.
     - Form floating "Lego blocks" from clues (e.g. [S above R], [V-T-P]).
   • Boundary & Height Limit:
     - Total height cannot exceed total box count N.
     - Anchor to top/bottom boundary conditions as early as possible.
   • Gap Formula:
     - Number of boxes between Pos X and Pos Y = |X - Y| - 1.

C. MONTH-DATE SCHEDULING METHODOLOGY (2-3 Months x 2 Dates):
   • Calendar Base Anchors:
     - 30-Day Months: April, June, September, November (AJSN - 4 months only).
     - 31-Day Months: January, March, May, July, August, October, December (7 months).
     - 28/29 Days: February.
   • Coordinates System:
     - Write columns: Month | Days | Odd Date (e.g. 11) | Even Date (e.g. 24).
   • The Slot Transition:
     - "Immediately before 11th April" = "24th March" (Previous chronological slot).
   • Odd-One-Out Grouping Traps:
     - 95% of bank exam grouping questions test either Month Day-Count (30 vs 31 days) or Date Parity (Odd vs Even).
