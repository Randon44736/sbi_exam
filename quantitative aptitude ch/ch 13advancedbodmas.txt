1. THE VBODMAS HIERARCHY:
   • V -> Vinculum (Bar bracket, e.g. 8 - 3 with a bar over it: solve first!)
   • B -> Brackets: Inner ( ) -> Curly { } -> Square [ ]
   • O -> "Of": Strictly means multiply, but takes precedence over normal division!
     TRAP ALERT: 40 ÷ 5 of 2 = 40 ÷ (5 x 2) = 40 ÷ 10 = 4 (NOT 8 x 2 = 16!).
   • D / M -> Division and Multiplication: Equal priority, solve STRICTLY LEFT TO RIGHT!
   • A / S -> Addition and Subtraction: Equal priority, solve STRICTLY LEFT TO RIGHT!

2. MIXED FRACTIONS WHOLE-PART SEPARATION RULE:
   • Never convert mixed fractions to improper fractions in speed math!
   • Formula:
     A b/c + D e/f - G h/k = (A + D - G) + (b/c + e/f - h/k)
   • Example: 7 1/4 + 5 1/2 - 3 1/4 = (7 + 5 - 3) + (1/4 + 1/2 - 1/4) = 9 + 1/2 = 9 1/2.

3. THE "WHOLE MINUS MIXED FRACTION" 3-SECOND TRICK:
   • To compute: Whole Number N - (A b/c):
     Step 1: Subtract 1 extra from whole number: (N - A - 1)
     Step 2: New numerator = (Denominator - Numerator): (c - b) / c
   • Example: 50 - 13 4/7:
     Step 1: 50 - 14 = 36
     Step 2: (7 - 4) / 7 = 3/7
     Answer: 36 3/7! (Zero rough paper needed!).

4. THE PERCENTAGE SWAP RULE (A% of B = B% of A):
   • When A% of B looks ugly, flip it!
   • Example: 64% of 25 = 25% of 64 = 64 ÷ 4 = 16!
   • Example: 48% of 50 = 50% of 48 = 48 ÷ 2 = 24!
   • Example: 36% of 250 = 250% of 36 = 2.5 x 36 = 90!

5. SURDS & INDICES BASE MATCHING LAW:
   • Whenever an equation has exponents (e.g. 4^? x 8^3 ÷ 16 = 2^10):
     Convert EVERY base to the smallest prime base (usually 2, 3, or 5)!
     - 4 = 2^2
     - 8 = 2^3
     - 16 = 2^4
     - 32 = 2^5
     - 64 = 2^6

[Q1.1] Mixed Fraction Separation Rule:
       Evaluate: 4 2/5 + 3 1/2 - 2 1/5 = ?
       [A] 4 7/10   [B] 5 3/10   [C] 5 7/10   [D] 6 1/10   [E] 5 1/2

       • Golden Separation Working:
         Step 1 (Whole numbers): 4 + 3 - 2 = 5.
         Step 2 (Matching denominators): 2/5 - 1/5 = 1/5.
         Step 3 (Cross-add): 1/5 + 1/2 = (2 + 5)/10 = 7/10.
         Total Combined = 5 7/10!
       • Exam Trap Avoided:
         Converting to improper fractions (22/5 + 7/2 - 11/5) creates big numerators and wastes 30+ seconds!
       • Answer: [C] 5 7/10 (Solved 100% correctly in seconds!).
