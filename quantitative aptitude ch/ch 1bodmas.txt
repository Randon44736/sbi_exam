1. RULES & CONCEPTS COVERED SO FAR
----------------------------------
A) VBODMAS Order of Operations:
   1. B -> Brackets: Solve inside-out: ( ) first, then { }, then [ ]
   2. O -> Of: Means multiply (x), done right after brackets (before divide)
   3. D / M -> Divide (÷) and Multiply (x): Go strictly LEFT TO RIGHT!
      * Trap fixed: 256 ÷ 16 x 4 = (256 ÷ 16) x 4 = 16 x 4 = 64 (NOT 256 ÷ 64)
   4. A / S -> Add (+) and Subtract (-): Go strictly LEFT TO RIGHT!
      * Trap fixed (-A + B): In 64 - 36 + 12, do NOT add 36 + 12 = 48.
        Think of money: Start with 64, spend 36 (= 28), get 12 (= 40).

B) Fractions — "of" (Multiply) vs "+" / "-" (Add / Subtract):
   - When you see "of" or "x":
     * NEVER cross-multiply!
     * Cancel matching numbers from top & bottom first, then multiply straight across.
     * Example: (1/3) of (3/4) of 40 -> cancel 3 with 3 -> (1/4) x 40 = 10.
     * Trap fixed (PDF Q1.7): (1/2) of (1/3) = (1/2) x (1/3) = 1/6 (NOT 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6).
   - When you see "+" or "-":
     * Top: Cross-multiply.
     * Bottom: MULTIPLY the bottom numbers (never add bottoms!).
     * Example: 1/2 + 1/3 = (3 + 2) / (2 x 3) = 5/6.
     * Example: 1/2 - 1/3 = (3 - 2) / (2 x 3) = 1/6.

C) Mixed Fractions (Whole + Fraction):
   - 4 1/5 means 4 + 1/5 (write with a space, not *).
   - Add whole numbers and fractions separately: 4 1/5 + 2 2/5 = 6 3/5.
   - Whole number minus mixed fraction (e.g., 10 - 2 1/3):
     1. Subtract 1 extra from whole: 10 - (2 + 1) = 7
     2. Fraction = (bottom - top) / bottom = (3 - 1)/3 = 2/3 -> Answer: 7 2/3.

3. COMPLETE STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTIONS & SHORTCUTS FOR REMAINING QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Q1.3] Evaluate: 3 1/3 + 4 2/5 - 2 1/2 = ?
       • Golden Rule: Separate whole numbers and fractions!
       • Whole numbers: 3 + 4 - 2 = 5.
       • Fractions: 1/3 + 2/5 - 1/2.
         LCM of (3, 5, 2) = 30.
         = (10 + 12 - 15) / 30 = 7/30.
       • Combined Answer: 5 7/30 (Option [A]).
       • Exam Trap: Do NOT convert to improper fractions (10/3 + 22/5 - 5/2); it wastes 40+ seconds!

[Q1.8] Evaluate (exact value): 115 - [64 ÷ {18 - (14 - 8)}] = ?
       • Step 1 (Innermost): 14 - 8 = 6.
       • Step 2 (Curly): 18 - 6 = 12.
       • Step 3 (Square): 64 ÷ 12 = 16/3 = 5 1/3.
       • Step 4 (Whole minus mixed fraction trick):
         115 - 5 1/3 = (115 - 6) + (1 - 1/3) = 109 2/3 = 329/3.
       • Answer: 329/3 or 109 2/3 (Option [B]).

[Q1.2] Evaluate (exact value): 45.6 ÷ [12.5 - (3.8 + 1.7)] = ?
       • Step 1 (Inner): 3.8 + 1.7 = 5.5.
       • Step 2 (Square): 12.5 - 5.5 = 7.0.
       • Step 3 (Division): 45.6 ÷ 7 = 456 / 70.
         Divide numerator and denominator by 2: 456/70 = 228 / 35.
       • Answer: 228/35 (Option [A]).

[Q1.4] 48% of 650 + 72% of 450 = ?
       • Golden Double-and-Halve Trick:
         48% of 650 = 24% of 1300 = 24 x 13 = 312.
         72% of 450 = 36% of 900 = 36 x 9 = 324.
       • Total = 312 + 324 = 636 (Option [A]).
       • Speed Tip: Doubling 650 to 1300 and 450 to 900 kills decimals mentally!

[Q1.6] (14.4 x 2.5) ÷ 0.6 + 18.5 = ?
       • Step 1: Divide first! 14.4 ÷ 0.6 = 144 ÷ 6 = 24.
       • Step 2: Multiply by 2.5 (Quarter of 24 x 10) = 60.
       • Step 3: Add 18.5 -> 60 + 18.5 = 78.5 (Option [A]).

[Q1.9] 0.04 x 0.025 x 1000 ÷ 0.05 = ?
       • Step 1: Pair 0.025 x 1000 first = 25.
       • Step 2: 0.04 x 25 = 1.0.
       • Step 3: 1 ÷ 0.05 = 100 ÷ 5 = 20 (Option [A]).

[Q1.10] 12^2 + 14^2 - 13^2 = ?
        • Golden Consecutive Squares Trick (a^2 - b^2 = a + b):
          14^2 - 13^2 = 14 + 13 = 27.
        • Now just add: 12^2 + 27 = 144 + 27 = 171 (Option [B]).
        • Time taken: 4 seconds!
