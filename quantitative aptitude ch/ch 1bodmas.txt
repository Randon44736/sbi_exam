1. RULES & CONCEPTS COVERED SO FAR
----------------------------------
A) VBODMAS Order of Operations:
   1. B -> Brackets: Solve inside-out: ( ) first, then { }, then [ ]
   2. O -> Of: Means multiply (x), done right after brackets (before divide)
   3. D / M -> Divide (÷) and Multiply (x): Go strictly LEFT TO RIGHT!
      * Trap fixed: 256 ÷ 16 x 4 = (256 ÷ 16) x 4 = 16 x 4 = 64 (NOT 256 ÷ 64)
   4. A / S -> Add (+) and Subtract (-): Go strictly LEFT TO RIGHT!
      * Trap fixed (-A + B): In 64 - 36 + 12, do NOT add 36 + 12 = 48.
        Think of money: Start with 64, spend 36 (= 28), get 12 (= 40).

B) Fractions — "of" (Multiply) vs "+" / "-" (Add / Subtract):
   - When you see "of" or "x":
     * NEVER cross-multiply!
     * Cancel matching numbers from top & bottom first, then multiply straight across.
     * Example: (1/3) of (3/4) of 40 -> cancel 3 with 3 -> (1/4) x 40 = 10.
     * Trap fixed (PDF Q1.7): (1/2) of (1/3) = (1/2) x (1/3) = 1/6 (NOT 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6).
   - When you see "+" or "-":
     * Top: Cross-multiply.
     * Bottom: MULTIPLY the bottom numbers (never add bottoms!).
     * Example: 1/2 + 1/3 = (3 + 2) / (2 x 3) = 5/6.
     * Example: 1/2 - 1/3 = (3 - 2) / (2 x 3) = 1/6.

C) Mixed Fractions (Whole + Fraction):
   - 4 1/5 means 4 + 1/5 (write with a space, not *).
   - Add whole numbers and fractions separately: 4 1/5 + 2 2/5 = 6 3/5.
   - Whole number minus mixed fraction (e.g., 10 - 2 1/3):
     1. Subtract 1 extra from whole: 10 - (2 + 1) = 7
     2. Fraction = (bottom - top) / bottom = (3 - 1)/3 = 2/3 -> Answer: 7 2/3.
