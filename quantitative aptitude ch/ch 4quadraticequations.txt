1. CORE SHORTCUTS & EXAM RULES
------------------------------
A) 2-Second Instant CND Rule (The Free Mark!):
   - If the constant term (c) in BOTH equations is NEGATIVE (-),
     the answer is ALWAYS [E] CND!
   - Real PDF Q4.2:
     I. x^2 + 2x - 35 = 0
     II. y^2 - 4y - 45 = 0
     Both end in minus (-35, -45) -> Instant [E] CND without solving!

B) Sign Table Shortcut (Roots Signs from Equation Signs):
   - Equation has (-, +) -> Roots are (+, +) (Both Positive)
   - Equation has (+, +) -> Roots are (-, -) (Both Negative)
   - If Eq I is (-, +) and Eq II is (+, +) -> Positive > Negative -> x > y instantly in 3 seconds!

C) Square vs Square Root Trap:
   - x^2 = 196 -> x = +14, -14 (Two roots!)
   - y = √196 -> y = +14 only (Principal root is positive!)
   - Comparison: {+14, -14} vs {+14} -> x <= y (Option D).

D) Negative Number Comparison Rule:
   - Smaller looking negative number is actually BIGGER in value!
   - Example: -5 > -9 (owing ₹5 is better than owing ₹9).
   - Real PDF Q4.5: x in {-6, -8} and y in {-9, -10} -> -6 > -9 and -8 > -9 -> x > y!

E) Standard Factoring Method:
   - x^2 - 11x + 30 = 0: Numbers that multiply to 30 and add to 11 -> 5 and 6. Roots: x = +5, +6.
   - y^2 - 15y + 56 = 0: Numbers that multiply to 56 and add to 15 -> 7 and 8. Roots: y = +7, +8.
   - Comparison: 5 and 6 are smaller than 7 and 8 -> x < y (Option B).
