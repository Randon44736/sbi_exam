## 📊 Rigorous Syllabus Audit (Current State)

### 1. What is Already Covered (Core Conceptual Foundation)
- **Ch 1:** VBODMAS precedence, basic fraction ops, "of" cancellation, decimal shifting.
- **Ch 2:** Clean rounding (.01–.49 vs .50–.99), benchmark % fractions, 5-second square root benchmark trick.
- **Ch 3:** Missing & wrong number series, constant & doubling differences, geometric ratios, cubes+1 pattern.
- **Ch 4:** 2-second Instant CND rule, Sign Transformation Matrix, $x^2$ vs $\sqrt{x}$ trap, basic factoring.
- **Ch 5:** Unit trick for age gaps, ratio partitioning of money/profit, price-consumption ladder ($1/n \rightarrow 1/(n+1)$), average shift of new member, consecutive numbers middle-number rule.
- **Ch 6:** Loss-to-profit % jump, Equal CP no-profit-no-loss rule, CI doubling powers rule.
- **Ch 7:** Total work LCM chocolate units, $5/18$ km/h to m/s conversion, boat downstream/upstream formulas.
- **Ch 8:** Cross-alligation, $\text{LCM} \times \text{HCF} = N_1 \times N_2$, 7-44-154 circle geometry scale.
- **Ch 9:** Tabular, Bar, Line, Pie degree ratios, Caselet & Missing DI structure.
- **Ch 10:** Quantity Comparison (Q1 vs Q2), Data Sufficiency 5 standard options and sufficiency rules.
- **Ch 20 (Covered earlier as Ch 11):** Probability definition, fast $\binom{n}{2}$ shortcut, word letter permutations.

### 2. What is Partially Covered (To Be Expanded in Part 2)
- **Ch 4:** Quadratic equations with fractional coefficients ($a > 1$), irrational roots ($\sqrt{3}$ roots), strict negative root comparisons.
- **Ch 6:** Multiple-transaction profit & loss, successive discounts, false-weight dishonest dealer variations, half-yearly ($R/2, 2T$) & quarterly ($R/4, 4T$) compounding.
- **Ch 7:** Alternate-day work with different rates, pipes with changing leakage rates, circular tracks & races.
- **Ch 8:** 3-component alligation, 3D volume recasting with melting loss.
- **Ch 9:** Missing DI with algebraic variables ($x, y$), double pie charts, mixed arithmetic DI.
- **Ch 10:** 3-statement data sufficiency, algebraic quantity comparisons.

### 3. What is Completely Missing (Newly Added Chapters 11–19, 21)
- **Ch 11:** Number System (Divisibility 2–13, remainder theorem, unit digit cyclicity, trailing zeros, factorials).
- **Ch 12:** Advanced Number Series (Double/triple diffs, Fibonacci, alternating $\times/\div$, fractional series).
- **Ch 13:** Advanced Calculation (Squares 1–50, cubes 1–20, surds & indices, multi-BODMAS).
- **Ch 14:** Advanced % & Proportion (Elections/voting, income-expenditure-savings, compound ratios).
- **Ch 15:** Advanced Average & Ages (Weighted average, average replacement, multi-condition age ratios).
- **Ch 16:** Advanced Time & Work (Men/Women/Children equivalence, work & wages, changing efficiency).
- **Ch 17:** Advanced Speed & Motion (Relative speed same/opposite, train crossing platform+pole, circular tracks).
- **Ch 18:** Advanced Mensuration & Geometry (Trapezium, cylinder, cone, hemisphere, melting & recasting).
- **Ch 19:** Advanced DI Sets (Variable DI, double pie, caselets, arithmetic DI, missing DI).
- **Ch 21:** Basic Trigonometry (Optional - sin/cos/tan definitions, standard angles, heights & distances).
