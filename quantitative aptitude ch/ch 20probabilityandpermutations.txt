1. PERMUTATION (ARRANGEMENT / ક્રમ મહત્વનો છે):
   • When order of selection matters (e.g. arranging letters in a word, seating in a row):
     nPr = n! / (n - r)!
   • Arranging 'n' distinct items in a straight row = n! ways.
     - 3 items (CAT) = 3! = 6
     - 4 items (BANK) = 4! = 24
     - 5 items = 5! = 120
     - 6 items = 6! = 720
     - 7 items = 7! = 5040
   • Repeating Letters Rule (Identical Items Trap):
     When letters repeat, DIVIDE by the factorial of each repetition count:
     Arrangements = (Total letters)! / (p! x q! x r!...)
     - "LEADER": 6 letters, E repeats twice -> 6! / 2! = 720 / 2 = 360 ways.
     - "DIRECTOR": 8 letters, R repeats twice -> 8! / 2! = 40320 / 2 = 20,160 ways.
   • "Vowels Together" (The Box / Grouping Method):
     Tie all vowels into ONE single bundle/box!
     Count the box as 1 item, find total arrangements, and then multiply by the internal arrangements inside the box!
   • "Never Together" Rule:
     Ways Never Together = (Total Possible Arrangements) - (Ways Always Together).

2. COMBINATION (SELECTION / પસંદગી - ક્રમ મહત્વનો નથી):
   • When order does NOT matter (e.g. choosing a committee, picking balls from a bag):
     nCr = n! / [r! x (n - r)!]
   • Fast Mental Calculation Shortcuts:
     - nC1 = n
     - nCn = 1
     - nC2 = [n x (n - 1)] / 2
       * 4C2 = (4 x 3)/2 = 6
       * 5C2 = (5 x 4)/2 = 10
       * 6C2 = (6 x 5)/2 = 15
       * 8C2 = (8 x 7)/2 = 28
       * 10C2 = (10 x 9)/2 = 45
       * 12C2 = (12 x 11)/2 = 66
     - nC3 = [n x (n - 1) x (n - 2)] / 6
       * 5C3 = (5 x 4 x 3)/6 = 10
       * 6C3 = (6 x 5 x 4)/6 = 20
       * 10C3 = (10 x 9 x 8)/6 = 120
       * 12C3 = (12 x 11 x 10)/6 = 220
     - Symmetry Rule: nCr = nC(n - r) (e.g. 10C8 = 10C2 = 45).

3. PROBABILITY FOUNDATIONS (સંભાવના):
   • Basic Law: P(Event) = (Number of Favorable Outcomes) / (Total Number of Possible Outcomes).
   • 0 <= P(Event) <= 1.
   • P(Happening) + P(Not Happening) = 1.
   • "At Least One" Master Hack:
     P(At least one) = 1 - P(None).
     NEVER calculate 1 + 2 + 3 separately! Find probability of zero occurrences, and subtract from 1!
   • "OR" vs "AND" Rules:
     - "AND" (Simultaneous / Both) -> MULTIPLY (x).
     - "OR" (Mutually exclusive alternatives) -> ADD (+).
   • Addition Rule for Non-Mutually Exclusive Events:
     P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B).

4. STANDARD PROBABILITY SAMPLE SPACES FOR BANKING EXAMS:
   • Coin Toss:
     - 1 coin: 2 outcomes {H, T}.
     - 2 coins: 2^2 = 4 outcomes {HH, HT, TH, TT}.
     - 3 coins: 2^3 = 8 outcomes {HHH, HHT, HTH, THH, HTT, THT, TTH, TTT}.
   • Rolling Dice:
     - 1 die: 6 outcomes.
     - 2 dice: 6 x 6 = 36 outcomes.
     - Prime sums from 2 dice: {2, 3, 5, 7, 11}. Total favorable pairs = 15 out of 36.
   • Standard Pack of 52 Playing Cards:
     - 26 Red cards (13 Hearts, 13 Diamonds) + 26 Black cards (13 Spades, 13 Clubs).
     - 4 Suits: Spades (♠), Hearts (♥), Diamonds (♦), Clubs (♣) — 13 cards each.
     - Face Cards (ચિત્રવાળા પાના): 12 cards total (4 Jacks, 4 Queens, 4 Kings).
     - Aces (એક્કા): 4 cards total.

[Q20.1] A bag contains 6 Red balls, 4 Blue balls, and 2 Green balls (12 balls total). If two balls are drawn at random, what is the probability that neither is Blue?
   ગુજરાતી: એક થેલીમાં 6 લાલ, 4 વાદળી અને 2 લીલા દડા છે. જો બે દડા યાદચ્છિક રીતે ખેંચવામાં આવે, તો એક પણ દડો વાદળી ન હોવાની સંભાવના કેટલી?
   [A] 14/33   [B] 1/3   [C] 7/33   [D] 19/33   [E] 5/11
   Answer: [A] 14/33
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Total balls = 6 + 4 + 2 = 12.
     • Total ways to draw 2 balls from 12 = 12C2 = (12 x 11) / 2 = 66.
     • Favorable: Neither ball is Blue -> balls must be drawn strictly from Red (6) + Green (2) = 8 non-blue balls!
     • Ways to draw 2 non-blue balls = 8C2 = (8 x 7) / 2 = 28.
     • Probability = 28 / 66 = 14 / 33.
   Easy Shortcut: Non-blue = 8. Fast formula: (8/12) x (7/11) = (2/3) x (7/11) = 14/33 in 5 seconds!
   Common Exam Trap: Never calculate Red-Red, Green-Green, Red-Green separately; lump all non-blue balls together into 8!

[Q20.2] A box contains 2 Blue, 4 Red, 5 Green, and 1 Yellow marbles (12 marbles total). If 3 marbles are drawn at random, what is the probability that exactly 2 are Red and 1 is Green?
   ગુજરાતી: એક પેટીમાં 2 વાદળી, 4 લાલ, 5 લીલી અને 1 પીળી લખોટીઓ છે. જો 3 લખોટીઓ પસંદ કરવામાં આવે, તો બરાબર 2 લાલ અને 1 લીલી હોવાની સંભાવના કેટલી?
   [A] 3/22   [B] 5/22   [C] 1/11   [D] 7/44   [E] 3/44
   Answer: [A] 3/22
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Total marbles = 2 + 4 + 5 + 1 = 12.
     • Total ways to choose 3 marbles = 12C3 = (12 x 11 x 10) / (3 x 2 x 1) = 220.
     • Favorable ways: Choose 2 Red out of 4 (4C2) AND 1 Green out of 5 (5C1).
       Favorable ways = 4C2 x 5C1 = [(4 x 3)/2] x 5 = 6 x 5 = 30.
     • Probability = 30 / 220 = 3 / 22.
   Easy Shortcut: 4C2 = 6, 5C1 = 5 -> Product = 30. Total 12C3 = 220. Ratio = 30/220 = 3/22. Done!
   Common Exam Trap: Do not forget to divide by 12C3 (some students divide by 12P3 or 12^3).

[Q20.3] Three fair coins are tossed simultaneously. What is the probability of getting at least two heads?
   ગુજરાતી: ત્રણ સિક્કા એકસાથે ઉછાળવામાં આવે છે. ઓછામાં ઓછા બે છાપ (Heads) મળવાની સંભાવના કેટલી?
   [A] 1/2   [B] 3/8   [C] 5/8   [D] 7/8   [E] 1/4
   Answer: [A] 1/2
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Total outcomes for 3 coins = 2^3 = 8:
       {HHH, HHT, HTH, THH, HTT, THT, TTH, TTT}.
     • "At least 2 heads" means either 2 Heads or 3 Heads:
       - Exactly 2 heads: {HHT, HTH, THH} = 3 outcomes.
       - Exactly 3 heads: {HHH} = 1 outcome.
       - Total favorable = 3 + 1 = 4 outcomes.
     • Probability = 4 / 8 = 1/2.
   Easy Shortcut: Symmetrical distribution: Total outcomes with >= 2 heads equals total outcomes with >= 2 tails = 8 / 2 = 4 -> 4/8 = 1/2.
   Common Exam Trap: "At least 2" INCLUDES 3 heads! Do not count only 2 heads (3/8 is wrong!).

[Q20.4] A basket of 12 oranges contains 4 rotten and 8 good oranges. If 3 oranges are picked at random, what is the probability that at least one orange is good?
   ગુજરાતી: 12 નારંગીની ટોપલીમાં 4 બગડેલી અને 8 સારી નારંગી છે. જો 3 નારંગી પસંદ કરવામાં આવે, તો ઓછામાં ઓછી એક સારી નારંગી મળવાની સંભાવના કેટલી?
   [A] 54/55   [B] 53/55   [C] 1/55   [D] 4/55   [E] 51/55
   Answer: [A] 54/55
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Use Golden Hack: P(At least 1 good) = 1 - P(None is good) = 1 - P(All 3 are rotten).
     • Total ways to choose 3 oranges out of 12 = 12C3 = 220.
     • Ways all 3 are rotten = choosing 3 from the 4 rotten = 4C3 = 4.
     • P(All 3 rotten) = 4 / 220 = 1 / 55.
     • P(At least 1 good) = 1 - (1 / 55) = 54 / 55.
   Easy Shortcut: Complement trick turns a complex 3-case problem into 1 simple fraction subtraction: 1 - 4/220 = 216/220 = 54/55 in 8 seconds!
   Common Exam Trap: Trying to calculate (1 good + 2 good + 3 good) individually takes 2 minutes and causes arithmetic mistakes.

[Q20.5] In how many different ways can the letters of the word "DIRECTOR" be arranged such that all the vowels are always together?
   ગુજરાતી: "DIRECTOR" શબ્દના અક્ષરોને એવી કેટલી રીતે ગોઠવી શકાય કે જેથી બધા જ સ્વરો (vowels) હંમેશા સાથે આવે?
   [A] 2,160   [B] 1,080   [C] 4,320   [D] 720   [E] 2,520
   Answer: [A] 2,160
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Word "DIRECTOR" has 8 letters:
       Consonants: D, R, C, T, R (5 consonants, R repeats 2 times).
       Vowels: I, E, O (3 distinct vowels).
     • Step 1 (Tie vowels in a box): Box = [I, E, O].
       Items to arrange = 5 consonants + 1 box = 6 items.
     • Step 2: Since R repeats 2 times, arrangements of these 6 items = 6! / 2! = 720 / 2 = 360.
     • Step 3: Vowels inside the box [I, E, O] can be arranged among themselves in 3! = 6 ways.
     • Total Ways = 360 x 6 = 2,160 ways.
   Easy Shortcut: (6! / 2!) x 3! = 360 x 6 = 2,160.
   Common Exam Trap: Forgetting that R repeats twice in DIRECTOR. Forgetting to divide by 2! gives 4,320 (Trap Option C).

[Q20.6] A card is drawn from a well-shuffled pack of 52 playing cards. What is the probability that the card drawn is either a King or a Spade?
   ગુજરાતી: 52 પત્તાની કેટમાંથી એક પત્તું ખેંચતા, તે પત્તું રાજા (King) અથવા કાળીનું (Spade) હોવાની સંભાવના કેટલી?
   [A] 4/13   [B] 17/52   [C] 16/52   [D] 1/4   [E] 9/26
   Answer: [A] 4/13
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Number of Kings in a deck = 4.
     • Number of Spades in a deck = 13.
     • King of Spades is common to both sets (Intersection = 1 card).
     • By Union Formula: Favorable cards = Kings + Spades - (King of Spades) = 4 + 13 - 1 = 16 cards.
     • Probability = 16 / 52 = 4 / 13.
   Easy Shortcut: 13 Spades already include 1 King. Just add the other 3 Kings: 13 + 3 = 16 cards -> 16/52 = 4/13!
   Common Exam Trap: Double-counting the King of Spades (4 + 13 = 17 -> 17/52 is a classic exam trap!).

[Q20.7] A committee of 5 members is to be formed from 6 men and 4 women. In how many ways can this be done if the committee must contain at least 3 men?
   ગુજરાતી: 6 પુરુષો અને 4 સ્ત્રીઓમાંથી 5 સભ્યોની સમિતિ બનાવવાની છે. જો સમિતિમાં ઓછામાં ઓછા 3 પુરુષો હોવા જોઈએ, તો આ કેટલી રીતે થઈ શકે?
   [A] 186   [B] 120   [C] 140   [D] 210   [E] 196
   Answer: [A] 186
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Total to choose = 5 members. "At least 3 men" means 3 Men, 4 Men, or 5 Men:
       Case 1: 3 Men and 2 Women = 6C3 x 4C2 = 20 x 6 = 120.
       Case 2: 4 Men and 1 Woman = 6C4 x 4C1 = 15 x 4 = 60.
       Case 3: 5 Men and 0 Women = 6C5 x 4C0 = 6 x 1 = 6.
     • Total Ways = 120 + 60 + 6 = 186 ways.
   Easy Shortcut: 6C3=20, 4C2=6 -> 120. 6C4=15, 4C1=4 -> 60. 6C5=6 -> 6. Sum = 120 + 60 + 6 = 186 in 15 seconds!
   Common Exam Trap: Forgetting Case 3 (all 5 men) gives 180, which is an incorrect choice!

[Q20.8] Two fair six-faced dice are thrown simultaneously. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers appearing on the top faces is a prime number?
   ગુજરાતી: બે પાસા એકસાથે ફેંકવામાં આવે છે. બંને પાસા પરના અંકોનો સરવાળો અવિભાજ્ય (Prime) સંખ્યા આવે તેની સંભાવના કેટલી?
   [A] 5/12   [B] 7/18   [C] 1/2   [D] 4/9   [E] 11/36
   Answer: [A] 5/12
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Total outcomes when two dice are rolled = 6 x 6 = 36.
     • Possible sums range from 2 to 12.
     • Prime sums are: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11:
       - Sum 2: (1,1) -> 1 pair
       - Sum 3: (1,2), (2,1) -> 2 pairs
       - Sum 5: (1,4), (2,3), (3,2), (4,1) -> 4 pairs
       - Sum 7: (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3), (5,2), (6,1) -> 6 pairs
       - Sum 11: (5,6), (6,5) -> 2 pairs
     • Total favorable pairs = 1 + 2 + 4 + 6 + 2 = 15 pairs.
     • Probability = 15 / 36 = 5 / 12.
   Easy Shortcut: Memorize the prime sum counts for 2 dice: 1 + 2 + 4 + 6 + 2 = 15. Probability = 15/36 = 5/12.
   Common Exam Trap: Missing (5,6) and (6,5) for 11, or counting 9 as a prime number.

[Q20.9] In how many different ways can the letters of the word "EQUATION" be arranged such that all the consonants are never together?
   ગુજરાતી: "EQUATION" શબ્દના અક્ષરોને એવી કેટલી રીતે ગોઠવી શકાય કે જેથી બધા જ વ્યંજનો (consonants) ક્યારેય સાથે ન આવે?
   [A] 36,000   [B] 33,600   [C] 40,320   [D] 28,800   [E] 30,240
   Answer: [A] 36,000
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • Word "EQUATION" has 8 distinct letters (5 vowels: E, U, A, I, O; 3 consonants: Q, T, N).
     • Total possible arrangements = 8! = 40,320.
     • Step 1 (Consonants Together): Tie [Q, T, N] into 1 box.
       Items = 5 vowels + 1 box = 6 items -> 6! = 720 ways.
       Inside box: 3 consonants arrange in 3! = 6 ways.
       Ways together = 720 x 6 = 4,320.
     • Step 2 (Never Together):
       Total arrangements - Consonants Together = 40,320 - 4,320 = 36,000 ways.
   Easy Shortcut: 8! - (6! x 3!) = 40,320 - 4,320 = 36,000 in 10 seconds!
   Common Exam Trap: Stopping after calculating 4,320 (that is consonants TOGETHER, not NEVER together).

[Q20.10] The probability that A speaks truth is 4/5, and the probability that B speaks truth is 3/4. What is the probability that they contradict each other when stating the same fact?
   ગુજરાતી: A સાચું બોલે તેની સંભાવના 4/5 છે અને B સાચું બોલે તેની સંભાવના 3/4 છે. કોઈ એક જ બાબત પર તેઓ પરસ્પર વિરોધાભાસી નિવેદન આપે (contradict કરે) તેની સંભાવના કેટલી?
   [A] 7/20   [B] 1/5   [C] 3/10   [D] 9/20   [E] 2/5
   Answer: [A] 7/20
   Step-by-Step (EN):
     • P(A speaks truth) = 4/5 ==> P(A lies) = 1 - 4/5 = 1/5.
     • P(B speaks truth) = 3/4 ==> P(B lies) = 1 - 3/4 = 1/4.
     • Contradiction happens when:
       Case 1: (A speaks truth AND B lies) OR Case 2: (A lies AND B speaks truth).
       P(Case 1) = (4/5) x (1/4) = 4/20.
       P(Case 2) = (1/5) x (3/4) = 3/20.
     • Total Probability = 4/20 + 3/20 = 7/20.
   Easy Shortcut: (4 x 1 + 1 x 3) / (5 x 4) = (4 + 3) / 20 = 7/20 in 5 seconds!
   Common Exam Trap: Forgetting Case 2 or multiplying 4/5 x 3/4 (which is the probability that they AGREE with each other).
