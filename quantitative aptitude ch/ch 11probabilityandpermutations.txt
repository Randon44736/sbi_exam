1. CORE CONCEPTS & SHORTCUT RULES
---------------------------------
A) Basic Probability Definition:
   - Probability P(E) = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes.
   - Example: Box with 4 Green + 6 Red = 10 balls total.
     P(Drawing 1 Green) = 4 / 10 = 2/5 (40%).

B) Fast Combination Shortcut for Picking 2 Items:
   - Formula: nC2 = [n x (n - 1)] / 2
   - Quick Values:
     * 4C2 = (4 x 3) / 2 = 6
     * 5C2 = (5 x 4) / 2 = 10
     * 6C2 = (6 x 5) / 2 = 15
     * 8C2 = (8 x 7) / 2 = 28
     * 10C2 = (10 x 9) / 2 = 45
     * 12C2 = (12 x 11) / 2 = 66

C) Drawing 2 Balls of Given Colors:
   - Example: Bag has 4 Red, 5 Green, Total 9 balls. Two balls picked.
     Total ways = 9C2 = (9 x 8)/2 = 36.
     Ways both are Red = 4C2 = (4 x 3)/2 = 6.
     Probability(Both Red) = 6 / 36 = 1/6.

D) "At Least One" Probability Rule:
   - P(At least one) = 1 - P(None).
   - Never calculate 1 + 2 + 3 separately!
   - Find probability of ZERO occurrences, and subtract from 1.

E) Word Letter Permutations (Arrangements):
   - Distinct letters: n! (e.g., "CAT" = 3! = 3 x 2 x 1 = 6 ways).
   - "BANK" = 4! = 24 ways.
   - With repeating letters: n! / (repeat_count)!
     * "MOON" (4 letters, O repeats twice) = 4! / 2! = 24 / 2 = 12 ways.
     * "LEADER" (6 letters, E repeats twice) = 6! / 2! = 720 / 2 = 360 ways.
