1. CORE SHORTCUTS COVERED
-------------------------
A) Rounding Rules:
   - .01 to .49 -> Round DOWN (18.02 -> 18, 14.05 -> 14)
   - .50 to .99 -> Round UP (19.98 -> 20, 3.99 -> 4, 124.95 -> 125)

B) Essential Benchmark Percentage Swaps:
   - 50% = 1/2 (Divide by 2)
   - 25% = 1/4 (Divide by 4)
   - 20% = 1/5 (Divide by 5)
   - 10% = 1/10 (Divide by 10)
   - 37.5% (or 37.49%) = 3/8 (Divide by 8, then multiply by 3)
   - 66.66% = 2/3 (Divide by 3, then multiply by 2)
   - 83.33% = 5/6 (Divide by 6, then multiply by 5)
   - 14.28% = 1/7, 28.57% = 2/7

C) 5-Second Square Root Method (Benchmarks + Unit Digit):
   - Benchmark Tens: 20^2=400, 30^2=900, 40^2=1600, 50^2=2500, 60^2=3600, 70^2=4900
   - Last digit matching:
     * Ends in 4 -> 2 or 8
     * Ends in 1 -> 1 or 9
     * Ends in 6 -> 4 or 6
     * Ends in 9 -> 3 or 7
     * Ends in 5 -> 5
   - Example: √1024 -> between 30^2 (900) & 40^2 (1600), ends in 4 -> 32 or 38.
     Since 1024 is close to 900 -> √1024 = 32.
   - Example: √1521 -> between 900 & 1600, ends in 1 -> 31 or 39.
     Since 1521 is very close to 1600 -> √1521 = 39.
