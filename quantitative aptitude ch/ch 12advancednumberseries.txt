SECTION 1: THE GOLDEN RULES & SPEED SHORTCUTS (COACH'S CHEAT SHEET)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE "TWO-SERIES" (PARALLEL PATTERN) MODEL (Super High-Yield in RRB PO Mains & Hard Prelims):
   • Structure: 
     Series I: N1, N2, N3, N4, N5, N6 (All numbers given, pattern is hidden).
     Series II: M, (a), (b), (c), (d), (e)... (Starts with new number M, follows SAME pattern).
   • Golden Strategy:
     Step 1: Crack the exact mathematical operation connecting consecutive terms in Series I.
     Step 2: Do NOT calculate all terms of Series II if an algebraic jump exists.
     Step 3: Apply the exact identified rule step-by-step from M to reach the asked letter (e.g., find 'c').

2. THE "DECIMAL MULTIPLIER" TRIGGER:
   • Clue: If the second number is roughly half (or slightly more/less than half) of the first number,
     IMMEDIATELY test multiplying by 0.5!
     - Model A: x0.5 + 0.5, x1 + 1, x1.5 + 1.5, x2 + 2...
     - Model B: x0.5, x1, x2, x4, x8... (Doubling multiplier)
     - Model C: x0.5 + 1, x1 + 2, x1.5 + 3...

3. DIFFERENCE OF DIFFERENCES (DOUBLE & TRIPLE DIFFERENCE):
   • When numbers grow slowly but single differences show no direct table, find the SECOND difference (D2).
   • If D2 is constant -> Quadratic pattern (n^2).
   • If D2 is increasing by +6, +12, +18... -> Cubic pattern (n^3).

4. WRONG NUMBER IDENTIFICATION (THE "TWO ADJACENT BAD GAPS" RULE):
   • If ONE number in the series is wrong, it ruins TWO consecutive differences!
   • The wrong number is ALWAYS the intersection point between the two flawed gaps.
   • The remaining differences will stay 100% consistent with the rule.

★ COACH'S 3-SECOND PATTERN IDENTIFICATION FLOWCHART:
HOW TO INSTANTLY SPOT ANY NUMBER SERIES PATTERN ON EXAM DAY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Check the First Number vs Last Number (The "Growth Rate" Test):
• Case A: Slow / Moderate Growth (e.g. starts at 10, ends around 50 to 120):
  ==> PURE DIFFERENCE SERIES!
  Calculate consecutive differences immediately on scratch paper.
  - Look for Squares: +4, +9, +16, +25, +36...
  - Look for Cubes: +1, +8, +27, +64, +125...
  - Look for Primes: +11, +13, +17, +19, +23... (Remember: 15, 21, 25, 27 are NOT prime!).
  - If single differences look random: Find Difference of Differences (D2)!

• Case B: Rapid / Explosive Growth (e.g. starts at 3, ends at 500, 1000, or 5000):
  ==> MULTIPLICATION SERIES!
  Never take differences! Look at the ratio between adjacent numbers:
  Estimate: Term2 / Term1, Term3 / Term2, Term4 / Term3.
  - Multiplier constant: x2, x2, x2 or x3, x3, x3.
  - Multiplier increasing: x1, x2, x3, x4, x5.
  - Multiplier with addition: (x1 + 1, x2 + 2, x3 + 3) or (x2 + 3, x2 + 3, x2 + 3).

• Case C: "The Half-Drop" (Term 1 -> drops to ~half -> then rises steadily):
  (e.g., 16 -> 9, 20 -> 11, 8 -> 5, 12 -> 7):
  ==> 100% GUARANTEED DECIMAL MULTIPLIER (x 0.5)!
  Write x0.5 in the first gap immediately! Pattern is almost always:
  (x0.5 + k, x1 + k, x1.5 + k, x2 + k) or (x0.5 + 0.5, x1 + 1, x1.5 + 1.5).

• Case D: "The Rollercoaster / Zig-Zag" (Up, Down, Up, Down):
  (e.g., 50, 45, 55, 40, 60...):
  ==> ALTERNATING SERIES!
  Split into two interleaved series (Odd positions: 1st, 3rd, 5th vs Even positions: 2nd, 4th, 6th)
  OR alternating single operations (+A, -B, +C, -D).

• Case E: "Sum of Previous Two" (Fibonacci):
  If differences match the previous terms themselves (e.g., 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21):
  Test: 1st + 2nd = 3rd? (2 + 3 = 5; 3 + 5 = 8; 5 + 8 = 13).

[Q1.2] Series I follows a doubling difference (+4, +8, +16, +32):
       Series I: 10, 14, 22, 38, 70
       Series II: 15, (a), (b), (c)...
       What is the value of (c)?
       [A] 39   [B] 41   [C] 43   [D] 45   [E] 47

       • Standard Working:

         (b) = 19 + 8 = 27

       • ★ SPEED HACK 1: THE "PARALLEL SHIFT" TRICK (2-SECOND ZERO-PEN SHORTCUT):
         - Compare the starting numbers of Series I and Series II:
           Shift = 15 - 10 = +5.
         - Since both series add the EXACT same differences (+4, +8, +16...),
           EVERY term in Series II is simply (Corresponding Term in Series I + 5)!
         - The 4th term in Series I is 38.
         - Therefore, 4th term (c) in Series II = 38 + 5 = 43! (Done in 2 seconds!).
         - NOTE: This Parallel Shift rule works whenever the pattern is ADDITIVE / DIFFERENCE based!

       • SPEED HACK 2: THE "TOTAL JUMP" SUM SHORTCUT:
         - To reach 4th term (c), you need 3 jumps: +4, +8, +16.
         - Total jump = 4 + 8 + 16 = 28.
         - Term (c) = 15 + 28 = 43!
