1. CORE PATTERNS & IDENTIFICATION CLUES
---------------------------------------
A) Slow Growth -> Addition / Difference Series (+):
   - Immediately calculate consecutive differences.
   - Example 1: 10, 18, 26, 34, 42 (+8 constant step)
   - Example 2 (Doubling difference): 10, 14, 22, 38, 70 (+4, +8, +16, +32)

B) Rapid Growth -> Multiplication Series (x):
   - Ratio between adjacent numbers is clean integers:
     4, 8, 24, 96, 480 (x2, x3, x4, x5)

C) Mixed Series -> "Multiply & Add" (x n + k):
   - Growth is faster than simple addition, but not pure multiplication.
   - Example: 3, 7, 16, 35, 74 (x2+1, x2+2, x2+3, x2+4)

D) Cubes & Squares Series (n^3 ± k or n^2 ± k):
   - Key Cubes to spot: 1^3=1, 2^3=8, 3^3=27, 4^3=64, 5^3=125, 6^3=216
   - Real PDF Q3.10: 2, 9, 28, 65, 126, 217 (Each is n^3 + 1)

E) Wrong Number Series:
   - Identify the uniform rule across the majority of terms.
   - The term that produces invalid differences on both its left and right is the wrong number.
   - Example: 10, 20, 30, 42, 50 -> 42 is wrong (should be 40).
