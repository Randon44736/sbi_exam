## Lesson 1 — Chapter 1: BODMAS, Fractions, Decimals & Exact Calculation

### 1. The Iron Rule: VBODMAS Precedence
Always follow this strict order:
**Brackets** `({[()]})` $\rightarrow$ **Of** (priority multiplication) $\rightarrow$ **Division** ($\div$) $\rightarrow$ **Multiplication** ($\times$) $\rightarrow$ **Addition** ($+$) $\rightarrow$ **Subtraction** ($-$)

> **Critical Trap:** $\div$ and $\times$ run **left-to-right** (do division before multiplication when $\div$ appears first).
> - **Wrong:** $256 \div 16 \times 4 \rightarrow 256 \div 64 = 4$
> - **Right:** $(256 \div 16) \times 4 = 16 \times 4 = 64$

### Step 1: BODMAS (What to calculate first?)

When you see a math line with `+`, `−`, `×`, `÷`, and `( )`, you **cannot** always solve left to right. Follow this simple priority list from top to bottom:

### Shortcut 1: The Alligation Cross-Subtraction (Real PDF Q8.1)

Whenever you mix a **cheaper** item and an **expensive** item to make an **average mixture**:
- Put the cheaper price on the left, expensive on the right, and target mixture price in the middle.
- **Cross-subtract** (bigger minus smaller):

$$\begin{array}{ccc}
\text{Cheaper Rice (₹48)} & & \text{Costly Rice (₹60)} \\
& \text{Mixture (₹52)} & \\
(60 - 52) = \mathbf{8} & & (52 - 48) = \mathbf{4}
\end{array}$$

- The mixing ratio is:
  $$8 : 4 = \mathbf{2 : 1}!$$
*(Done in 5 seconds!)*

### Shortcut 2: The Universal LCM & HCF Law (Real PDF Q8.3)

> **The Golden Law:**
> $$\mathbf{\text{Number } 1 \times \text{Number } 2 = \text{HCF} \times \text{LCM}}$$

Look at **Real PDF Q8.3**:

- $\text{LCM} = 240$
- First Number $= 48$
- Find the second number:
  $$48 \times N_2 = 12 \times 240$$

### Shortcut 3: The Famous "7 – 44 – 154" Circle Shortcut (Real PDF Q8.6)

In bank exams, circle radii are almost ALWAYS multiples of **$7$** because $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$!

- **Radius ($r$)** $= \mathbf{7\text{ cm}}$

*(No need to write $\frac{22}{7} \times 14 \times 14$ on rough paper!)*
