## 🧠 HITENDRA'S MASTERED TRICKS & AVOIDED TRAPS

1. **BODMAS Trap Overcome:**
   - In $64 - 36 + 12$, do NOT add $36 + 12$ first! Treat as $+64 - 36 + 12 = 40$.
   - Left-to-right rule for $\div$ and $\times$: $256 \div 16 \times 4 = 16 \times 4 = 64$.
2. **Fraction Trap Overcome:**
   - `"of"` means multiplication and cancellation top-to-bottom.
   - `"+"` and `"-"` require multiplying denominators and cross-multiplying numerators.
3. **Approximation Confidence:**
   - Always round to nearest clean whole number first: $89.46 \approx 89$, $63.92 \approx 64$, $19.99 \approx 20$.
4. **Quadratic Equations 2-Second Super Hacks:**
   - **Both constant signs negative ($-\text{c}_1, -\text{c}_2$):** Instant **[E] CND** without solving!
   - **Sign rule:** $(- , +) \implies (+ , +)$ and $(+ , +) \implies (- , -)$. Positive always beats negative $\implies x > y$.
   - **Roots:** $x^2 = 196 \implies x = \pm 14$, but $y = \sqrt{196} \implies y = +14$. Comparing $\{+14, -14\}$ to $\{+14\} \implies x \le y$.
   - **Negative numbers:** $-6 > -9$ (owing less is having more value).
